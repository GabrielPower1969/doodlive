# Real Miracles — the roof and the storm (story pack)

Title: **真正的神迹 / Real Miracles**. Id `miracles`, script prefix `mi`, env prefix `MI_`, set `galilee`, line ids
`mi_01…mi_33`.

Source: Mark 2:1-12 (Luke 5:17-26) and Mark 4:35-41 (public-domain text). Every line is our own child-friendly
paraphrase with its verse; nothing is quoted from any film or translation. Audience 4-10. A reference viewing lent only
costume logic, set furniture and light per scene (see DESIGN.md); characters, words, set and music are original.

One-sentence moral: **Jesus's miracles are not magic tricks: they show who He is — He loves us and He can help us.**

## Child-friendly choices (do not undo)

- The reference episode's demon story (the man in the tombs and the pigs, Mark 5) is left out: too frightening for
  four-year-olds. The parable of the sower is left out too (one pack, two miracles).
- The teachers of the law who grumble (Mark 2:6-7) are one narrator sentence at most; no villain on stage. Jesus's
  answer keeps its point in a child's words ("which is easier... so that you know").
- The storm is exciting, never horror: a few seconds of real danger, then comic panic (Peter bailing with a bucket).
  Nobody falls in.
- Peter's roof is the comedy: dust on his head, "My roof!", and at the end he laughs about it.
- The moral says plainly what the episode's frame story says: a magic trick fools the eyes; a real miracle comes from
  God and shows who Jesus is.

## Writing rules (same as Ruth / Daniel / Jonah)

Said aloud to a child; people talk to each other, the narrator bridges; one light moment per act (the bounce off the
crowd, the dust on Peter, the wobbly first steps, the dance with the mat, Peter's bucket); every line keeps its verse
reference.
