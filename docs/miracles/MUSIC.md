# Miracles — music and sound effects

Generated by `tools/story/music_miracles.py` from the timeline (see "Which timeline"). Do not hand-edit the audio
files; change the script and rerun it:

```
python3 tools/story/music_miracles.py                          # music + sfx + preview + the tables below
python3 tools/story/music_miracles.py --skip-music --skip-sfx  # preview only (e.g. after new voice clips)
python3 tools/story/music_miracles.py --only storm,calm         # re-render some cues, keep the others' rows
python3 tools/story/music_miracles.py --stems                  # also print per-stem levels (for balancing)
python3 tools/story/music_miracles.py --spec /tmp/mi_spec      # also write a spectrogram PNG of every output
```

Same toolchain as the other packs (`mido numpy scipy`, `fluid-synth`, `ffmpeg`, macOS `gs_instruments.dls`); imports
`music_kit.py` and `music_polish.py` without changing them; the loop / build machinery is copied from `music_jonah.py`.
A full run takes about one minute. Scratch: `/tmp/miracles_music/` (emptied per cue, removed at exit). Seeds are per
cue / effect (`miracles/<id>`), so reruns are identical. Engine and licence: our own synthesis (FluidSynth + the
macOS DLS bank, numpy DSP), as in every pack (R7). The tables between the `<!-- …:start/end -->` markers are rewritten
by the script.

## Listen first

Nobody has listened yet; everything below was judged by measurement (per-cue 1 s RMS profiles, band energy, centroid,
spectrograms) and the voice-over-bed model. Please listen in this order:

1. **`storm` into `calm` (zh 141 – 180 s in `preview_mix.mp3`)**: must feel exciting, never scary; the gallop stops on
   "Wind, stop!" and holds an A chord; then `calm` must read as a *sudden hush* (measured: storm's last second
   −23 dB RMS, calm's first second −33 dB, then the still chord). Check the two thunder hits sit on timpani + crash.
2. **`no_way`**: the comic gestures (tuba "bonk" on `boing`, tick-tock + "thinking" clarinet, celesta "idea" on
   `ding`). The likeliest to read wrong without a listen. In the **English play the `ding` lands 2.9 s after the
   music's idea arpeggio** (the cue is composed for the zh timing; see "Hits").
3. **`heal`**: the Jesus theme on solo flute, then the swell after "Get up!" (the loudest moment of the score,
   −12 dB RMS for ~2 s at 25 s into the cue, right after the line and before the man's "I'm standing!").
4. **`joy` and `finale`**: the friends theme (oud, major) and the Jesus theme (flute) *at the same time*. If they
   clash, the JOINT harmony bar 2 (A7 under the friends' G-A-F#) is the place to fix.
5. **The "oud"**: it is the GM nylon guitar, low-passed with tremolo on long notes. If it sounds like a Spanish guitar
   rather than Galilee, that is a bank limit (see "Weak").
6. **`roof`**: the rising sequence, the digging rhythm against the `dig_loop` SFX, and Peter's "My roof!" stop.
7. **SFX `crowd_murmur`, `rope_creak`, `dig_loop`**: synthesised textures; judge whether they read as a crowd, a rope,
   digging.
8. `preview_mix.mp3` start to end for balance (zh play, mixed as the engine mixes it).

## Which timeline

Cues are **composed for the play as the phone runs it in Chinese** (`voice_margin.lang_timeline("miracles", "zh")`,
the timeline re-timed to the installed zh clips; 198.9 s at the last run). It is the shortest play, so no cadence is
cut off by the next cue. In-cue hits are looked up by event (below). Each file is long enough for its **longest**
section across the zh play, the en play and `assets/miracles/timeline.json` (226.9 s at the last run, the longer clip per
line) × 1.3 + 0.6 s (`longestSpan` in `cues.json`); past its cadence each cue continues into a quiet
loop section on its final chord (`[loopStart, loopEnd)`, sample-exact, identical tiles), so a longer section loops or
just plays on. **Rerun this script after any re-time** (the Voice role was still adding English at the last run).

## Formats (CONTRACT section 7)

- `assets/miracles/audio/music/<cue>.wav` and `audio/sfx/<id>.wav`: 44.1 kHz, mono, 16-bit PCM, plus a 96 kbps mono
  `.mp3` next to each (use the WAV lengths for timing).
- Music: the composed span at −20.5 LUFS (EBU R128, ffmpeg `ebur128`), with a little dynamic range inside the
  contract's −20 ±1: `lowering` and `calm` −20.9, `joy` −19.6, `storm` −19.4. Loop beds 4.5 dB under their cue. True
  peak ≤ −1 dBTP (measured −2.2 … −6.7). Below 45 Hz removed; a speech pocket (−1.5 dB at 900 Hz, −3.5 dB at
  2.4 kHz); the score sits 7 dB down under the story and swells back on its hits (`music_polish.underscore`).
- SFX: sample peak −3.0 dBFS; `gainDb` in `mix.json` is the recommended playback gain.
- `assets/miracles/audio/preview_mix.mp3`: the zh play (33/33 lines) with music and SFX as `StoryDirector` mixes them.

## The two themes

Both original (`FRIENDS` and `JESUS` in the script) and written to fit together (`JOINT` harmony).

- **Friends theme**: bouncy, determined, 4 bars in D mixolydian: D-F#-A A-B-A / G-A-F# E-F#-D / D-F#-A-D C-A /
  B-A-G-E D. The C natural is the folk colour; `friends(major=True)` makes it C# for `joy` and `finale`. Oud over
  handclaps and frame drum, a "heave-ho" cello (long on 1, short kick on the "and" of 2).
- **Jesus theme**: calm, warm, rising, 8 bars in D major: A-B-D / E…D / F#-E-D-B / A-B-D, then higher: A-B-D / G…F# /
  E-D-E-F# / D. Flute and strings. Heard in `capernaum` (end), `heal` (in full), `evening` (6/8, low flute in G),
  `calm` (very soft), `joy` and `finale` (with the friends theme).

## Sound effects

`wind_loop` is a whole-file seamless loop (wrap-folded with a 0.6 s equal-power fold); it stops at cue `calm`
(`wind_stop` is the one-shot that ends it). **`dig_loop` is a one-shot** (lead decision 2026-11-18): `StoryDirector` can
stop a loop only when a music cue starts and `roof` runs on through S05, so the file is sized from its start to S05's
start in the zh play + 0.5 s (5.31 s), fading in over 0.3 s and out over the last 0.6 s. In the en play S05 comes
0.45 s later, so the digging ends just as the shot changes. The id keeps its contract name.

## Weak or unverified

- **Nobody has listened.** All judgements are measurements; see "Listen first".
- **The oud is a nylon guitar** from the GM bank (no oud in `gs_instruments.dls`); colour comes from the mode,
  tremolo and frame drum rather than the timbre. A real oud sample is not allowed without a licence check (R7).
- **English timing**: composed for the zh play. `ding` drifts +2.9 s in the en play (the idea arpeggio arrives before
  "I've got it!"); other hits drift ≤ 0.5 s. The en play's longer sections are covered by the loop beds.
- **`dig_loop`** is sized to the zh timing; if mi_10 / mi_11 or the S04b staging change, re-run `music_miracles.py --skip-music`.
- **Low end on phones**: `storm` has 60 % of its energy in 60-250 Hz and `thunder` 87 % below 150 Hz; phone
  speakers will thin both. Not changed (the jonah thunder was accepted the same way).
- **Very little above 4 kHz** in every cue (dark, warm bank + low-passes). Intended, but may sound dull on a good
  speaker.
- **Re-timing**: rendered against the zh play of 2026-11-18 00:5x (198.9 s) and the base timeline at 226.9 s. If the
  Voice role changes zh clips again, rerun the script (hits follow the events; files are sized with a 30 % margin).
