# Jonah — Jonah and the Big Fish (story pack)

Title: **约拿与大鱼 / Jonah and the Big Fish**. Id `jonah`, script prefix `jn`, env prefix `JN_`, line ids `jn_01…jn_29`.

Source: the Book of Jonah, chapters 1-4 (public-domain text). Every line is our own child-friendly paraphrase with
its verse; nothing is quoted from any film or translation. Audience 4-10. A reference viewing lent only costume
logic, set furniture and light per scene (see DESIGN.md); characters, words, set and music are original.

One-sentence moral: **God loves every person and gives second chances — to Nineveh, to Jonah, to you.**

## Child-friendly choices (do not undo)

- The sailors' lots and their fear of dying are left out; the captain is kind and does not want to throw Jonah in.
- No drowning imagery: Jonah sinks for one narrator sentence and the fish is already waiting.
- The fish is comic (gulp, burp, "pfft", waves a fin). Inside is "dark, stinky, slimy", never scary.
- The king of Nineveh is one phrase ("from the king to the smallest child"); no sackcloth-and-ashes scene.
- Jonah's wish to die (4:3, 4:8) becomes sulking and wailing about the plant.
- The book ends on God's question; the narrator adds one sentence of moral.

## Writing rules (same as Ruth / Daniel)

Said aloud to a child; people talk to each other, the narrator bridges; one light moment per act (the tiptoe, the
snore, the burp, the seaweed, the sulk, the worm); every line keeps its verse reference.
