# Joseph — Joseph the Dreamer (story pack)

Title: **约瑟的梦 / Joseph the Dreamer**. Id `joseph`, script prefix `js`, env prefix `JS_`, line ids `js_01…js_31`.

Source: Genesis 37 and 39-46, with 50:20 (public-domain text). Every line is our own child-friendly paraphrase with
its verse; nothing is quoted from any film or translation. Audience 4-10. A reference viewing lent only costume
logic, set furniture and light per scene (see DESIGN.md); characters, words, set and music are original.

One-sentence moral: **God was with Joseph in the well, the prison and the palace, and He can turn bad things into good.**

## Child-friendly choices (do not undo)

- The brothers do not plan to kill him; no blood on the coat; the lie to Jacob is not shown (Jacob only weeps).
- Potiphar's wife is "someone told a lie about him". The cupbearer and the baker are left out.
- The silver cup, Simeon as hostage and Benjamin's test are left out: the brother bows, Joseph asks about their
  father and reveals himself.
- One `brother` stands for all ten; the narrator says "the brothers".
- 50:20 ("you meant it for harm, God meant it for good") is spoken at the reveal: it is the heart of the story.

## Writing rules (same as Ruth / Daniel)

Said aloud to a child; people talk to each other, the narrator bridges; one light moment per act (the twirl, the
brother's "Dream on!", Pharaoh's "cows eating cows?"); every line keeps its verse reference.
