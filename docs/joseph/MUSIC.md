# Joseph — music and sound effects

Generated by `tools/story/music_joseph.py` from `assets/joseph/timeline.json` re-timed to the zh clips (see "Re-timed play and voice clarity"; finishing helpers in `tools/story/music_polish.py`, the voice-over-bed measurement in `tools/story/voice_margin.py`). Do not hand-edit the audio files; change
the script and rerun it:

```
python3 tools/story/music_joseph.py                          # music + sfx + preview + the tables below (~3.5 min)
python3 tools/story/music_joseph.py --skip-music --skip-sfx  # preview only
python3 tools/story/music_joseph.py --stems                  # also print per-stem target levels
python3 tools/story/music_joseph.py --spec /tmp/joseph_spec  # also write a spectrogram PNG of every output
```

Toolchain as the other packs (`mido numpy scipy`, `fluid-synth`, `ffmpeg`, macOS `gs_instruments.dls`); the script imports
`tools/story/music_kit.py` and does not change it. Scratch: `/tmp/joseph_music/`, removed at exit. Seeds are per cue / effect
(`joseph/<id>`). Tables between the `<!-- …:start/end -->` markers are rewritten by the script. **The timeline is timed by the
real voices; if it is re-timed, rerun the script** (every in-cue hit is looked up by event; asserts check every cue / sfx id
has a composer and every file is at least span x 1.3 + 0.6 s).

## Listen first

Nobody listened before this was built; checks are measurements and spectrograms. Please listen in this order:

1. **`iam` from 192.9 s zh (release ~200.6 s)** ("I am Joseph"): the release must land on "我就是约瑟" (estimated from the line length, D-level; if
   it is early or late, change `.55` in `cue_iam`). Before it the theme is deliberately cut short (cello, flute stop on D).
2. **`harp_gliss` + the cues around it** (24.7, 36.7, 100.6, 113.9, 183.8 s zh): the SFX is 3.6 s; the cues play bells only after it. Check
   the first three clouds sound like one motif and the `pharaoh` one (113.9 s) sounds *strange*, not broken.
3. **`pharaoh` 99-124 s**: is the march funny rather than loud? Snoring bent bassoon in the first bar.
4. **`betrayal`**, last 6 bars: minor theme on English horn; the final empty fifth under Jacob's weeping.
5. **`prison` at ~90.6 s** ("you are still with me"): the pad turning to G major and the flute entering; the entry is
   estimated from the line length, not detected.
6. **`plenty` at the fanfare (152.2 s)**: SFX and cue chord are both G major and should reinforce each other.
7. **SFX `sheep_baa`, `camel_grunt`, `moo`, `cell_door`** (creature sounds are the weakest synthesis, pitfall M4); the
   door is the same file for shut (83.5 s) and open (123.7 s).
8. `preview_mix.mp3` start to end: the zh play mixed as the engine mixes it (music 0.8 x duck 0.5, SFX undocked).
9. The new level work: is the score still warm and present in the gaps, and do the swells on the hits still feel big while
   the underscore sits behind the voice? (`DIPS` and the 7 dB underscore are the knobs.)

## Formats (CONTRACT section 7)

- `assets/joseph/audio/music/<cue>.wav`, `audio/sfx/<id>.wav`: 44.1 kHz mono 16-bit, plus a 96 kbps `.mp3` each.
- Music −20.9 LUFS over the composed span (contract −20 ±1, at its quiet end since 2026-11-15), loop bed −25.4, true peak ≤ −1 dBTP, nothing below 45 Hz. SFX sample peak −3.0 dBFS;
  `gainDb` in `mix.json` is the recommended playback gain.
- Every cue continues after its cadence (`loopFromSec`) into a quiet bed on its final chord; `[loopStart, loopEnd)` is one
  sample-identical repeat of at least 4 s, so looping equals playing straight on. Without looping every file runs ≥ 30 %
  past its span and fades over its last 2.5 s. `wind_desert` is a whole-file seamless loop (`stopAtCue: brothers`).

## The two themes

**Joseph theme** (G major, 8 bars; `JOSEPH`, `JOSEPH_MIN`, `JOSEPH_34`): up G-B-D-E, a step lower E-G-C-E, climbing to the
tonic. Shepherd's flute. `home` (full), `dream` (bars 1-2, soft), `betrayal` (G minor, English horn, ends on an empty fifth),
`prison` (bars 1-2 at "still with me"), `plenty` (bars 1-3, 8, trumpet dance), `reveal` (bars 5-6), `brothers` (answers the
bells, then bars 1-2 warm), `iam` (held back, then full strings), `reunion` (3/4 waltz), `finale` (all 8 bars).

**Dream motif**: rising harp glissando over G lydian (G3-G6) + three bells D6-G6-B6. The SFX `harp_gliss` is exactly that
and is one file (5 uses: 29.2, 41.4, 109.4, 125.6, 198.6 s). Cues never play a glissando on top of it; they answer with the
bells (`dream`, `brothers`), warp it (`pharaoh`: whole-tone pad, bent bells D-Ab-B, sliding trombone) or put it on top of the
theme (`finale`).

## How the score is made

As Jonah: MIDI per instrument through FluidSynth, mixed in numpy with a synthetic hall, tempo stretched so whole bars fill
each span, NRPN sustain fix for winds / strings / brass / voices. **Levels:** instead of Jonah's fixed `TRIM` table, every
stem is measured over the stretches where it plays and scaled to `REF_DB + gain_db`, so `gain_db` in a cue means "how loud
this part is while playing" (-2 lead, -6 pad). Hits are placed by timeline events (sparkle, coins, cell door, fanfare,
heart glow, `harp_gliss`), never hard-coded times.

## Re-timed play and voice clarity (2026-11-15 pass)

**What the cues are timed to.** `assets/joseph/timeline.json` is timed to the *longer* of the zh / en clip per line (270.5 s), but
the phone retimes at load to the language's own clips (`StoryRetime`, anchors). The Chinese play is 247.7 s, the English
263.5 s. The script therefore builds the **zh re-timed play** (`voice_margin.lang_timeline`, = `build_timeline.build`
with the zh clip lengths) and composes every cue span and every hit for it; `cues.json` `t` / `span` are zh times. Hits are
looked up by event as before. The English play uses the same cue files: where its events fall at a different place in the
cue, the hit is early or late by the drift below (a cue is never composed twice; the loop bed covers a longer section, a
shorter section cuts the last phrase into the 0.6 s crossfade). Nothing was composed for the old 233 s / 270 s timeline.

**Why the voices were buried, and what changed.** From `StoryDirector.cs` (read 2026-11-15): a line plays at volume 1; music
= file x 0.8 x duck, duck 1 -> 0.5 while a clip plays (music is 8 dB under speech); **SFX one-shots and the SFX loop are
not ducked** (they play at `gainDb` on top of the voice; `sfxLoop` has no duck, and `fadeInSec` in `mix.json` is not read).
Cues are held at -20 +-1 LUFS by `check.py`, so the music cannot simply be turned down; the pass therefore:

- renders every cue at **-20.9 LUFS** (was -20.0; the contract floor is -21.0) and the loop bed at -4.5 dB under the span
  (was -3): `SPAN_LUFS`, `VAMP_REL_DB`;
- carves a **speech pocket** into every cue (-1.5 dB at 900 Hz, -3.5 dB at 2.4 kHz, wide): `music_polish.pocket`;
- shapes the score to **breathe**: `music_polish.underscore` holds it 7 dB under the story and lifts it only around its
  hits (the SFX / glows in `HIT_SFX` / `HIT_FX`), so after normalisation the underscore is quieter and the hits stay big;
- adds a few shallow **dips** under lines the score cannot get out of the way of (`DIPS`, picked from the lowest lines of the table below);
- fades every mid-story cue in over 0.3 s so it never cuts in on a note;
- shortens the SFX that sit on speech (`SFX_GAIN` 2-4 dB lower, `SFX_SHAPE` short tails: chime, thunder, splash, bubbles,
  gliss, door ...); the punchy front keeps its level.

**Measured** with `python3 tools/story/voice_margin.py joseph` (engine model, zh play; voice RMS over its active frames
against music + SFX in the same frames, 100 Hz - 6 kHz). It is a model, not the reviewer's cross-correlation residual
(which has a codec floor): on this scale Daniel's pack is median 12.2 dB, worst 8.2 (reviewer scale 8.3 / 5.3).
"Before" = the old cue files played at the re-timed times, as they were this morning.
