1080×2400, the demo's aspect) projected every actor's head and feet and the dream panels at each second. It reproduced
the review on the old timeline (S06 pair at ndc x +0.57 / +0.87, S07 everyone off screen, S03 Jacob at −0.70, the

measured +0.56 / +0.18). The cause of three findings is in the engine's camera rules, not in the models:
`"all"` averages every actor (also parked ones), and `"fixed"` freezes the target where it is at the shot's start.
