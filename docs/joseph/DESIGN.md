# Joseph — art direction (costume, make-up, props, set, light, music)

Same family as Ruth and Daniel: Paper & Crayon look, rounded readable shapes, decal faces, same head-to-body ratio.
**Every colourable region is a closed shape ≥ 6 mm on the A4 print.** Small parts (sandals, cords, eyes, jewellery
studs) are fixed-colour detail lines, not regions. All designs are original; the reference viewing lent only the
logic below.

Period: patriarchal Canaan (tent-dwelling shepherds) and Middle-Kingdom Egypt.

## 1. Characters

### Joseph (hero) — height 0.34. One model, three looks, switched by props

- **Face**: young, open, friendly; big dark eyes, soft brows, **dark-brown hair to the jaw with a centre parting**,
  no beard (clean-shaven in both lands, which also keeps him one model).
- **Base costume** (always there): a plain **cream sleeveless tunic** to the knee, a thin brown cord belt, sandals.
- **Look A, the coat** (`coat` prop, S01-S03): a knee-length open robe with wide sleeves in **five broad vertical
  stripes**, each its own colour region (`coat1`…`coat5`, each stripe ≥ 6 mm on the sheet): red, yellow, green,
  blue, purple in the demo. A gold edging line (fixed colour). **The sheet prints Joseph wearing the coat**: it is
  what the child colours.
- **Look B, plain** (S03-S06): the base tunic only.
- **Look C, governor** (`nemes` + `collar` props, S07 on): a striped Egyptian head-cloth with two lappets on the
  chest and a broad round collar. They are hidden on the sheet, so **they take the child's coat colours**: the
  head-cloth's stripes alternate `coat2` / `coat4`, the collar's three rings are `coat1`, `coat3`, `coat5`. The coat
  that was stolen comes back as his crown; say this in the pack's SET.md / RIG.md so nobody "fixes" it.
- **Expressions**: `bright` (default), `talk`, `joy`, `scared`, `sad`, `pray` (eyes closed), `wise` (calm, small
  smile), `moved` (wet eyes), `tears_joy`.
- **Regions**: skin, hair, tunic, coat1-coat5.

### Jacob (father) — height 0.33 (stooped; 0.35 straight)
- **Face**: very old, long **white beard and hair**, bushy white brows, kind wrinkles.
- **Costume**: a long **grey-brown** robe, a wide **cream shawl** over head and shoulders, a brown belt, a
  **walking stick** (in the right hand, always).
- **Expressions**: `fond` (default), `talk`, `joy`, `weep` (eyes shut, tears), `tears_joy`.
- **Regions**: skin, beard+hair, robe, shawl, belt, stick.

### The brother (Judah) — height 0.38
- **Face**: about thirty, broad, strong jaw, short **black beard**, heavy brows that scowl well; later the same face
  must look worn and sorry.
- **Costume**: a rough **rust-brown** knee-length tunic, a **dark-olive** sleeveless shepherd's over-vest (sheepskin
  look, shaggy edge as a fixed line), a wide leather belt, a **sling bag** across the chest, sandals. Bare arms.
- **Expressions**: `sour` (default), `talk`, `angry`, `greedy` (S03, a sly side look), `humble`, `shock`, `sorry`
  (tears), `relief`.
- **Regions**: skin, beard+hair, tunic, vest, belt, bag.

### Pharaoh — height 0.40
- **Face**: forty, smooth, clean-shaven with a small **ceremonial chin beard**, **kohl-lined eyes**, arched brows.
- **Costume**: the **double crown** simplified to one tall rounded red-and-white crown with a gold band; a broad
  gold-and-blue collar; bare chest; a white pleated kilt with a long **blue-and-gold front apron**; gold armbands;
  sandals. Round belly, proud posture.
- **Expressions**: `grand` (default), `talk`, `sleepy`, `panic`, `curious`, `delighted`.
- **Regions**: skin, crown (red), crown (white), collar, kilt, apron.

## 2. Set: `twolands` (one page, two countries)

Left half = Canaan. Right half = Egypt. A strip of dunes with a caravan road joins them across the middle.

**Canaan (left)**
- **Tent** (back left): a big brown-and-black striped goat-hair tent, open front, poles, guy ropes, a rug, jars.
- **Sheep**: three woolly sheep near the tent (static; two heads may bob in a looping action).
- **Dry well** `pit` (front left): a ring of rough stones, wall height 0.08, with a rope and a bucket beside it.
- **Olive tree** and a low stone sheep-fold.
- **Caravan** (prop, hidden until S03): one camel with a striped saddle-cloth and bundles, and a small hooded
  trader figure beside it (static; part of the set, fixed colours allowed on the trader).

**Egypt (right)**
- **Palace front** (back right): a raised dais with four steps, two fat papyrus-bud columns painted in bands, a
  winged sun-disc over the lintel, a **throne**, two tall fans, a fire bowl on a stand.
- **Granary** (front right): three beehive-shaped mud-brick grain stores with little doors and a ladder;
  `grainPiles` (golden heaps and full baskets, hidden until S07); `sacks` (three tied grain sacks, hidden until S09).
- **Prison cell** `cell` (prop, hidden except S04-S06): a small open-topped cage of wooden bars, 0.16 square, with
  a stone bench and a clay jug.
- Two date palms; a row of small pyramids on the horizon relief (back wall).

**The four dream clouds** (props, hidden; each floats at h ≈ 0.46 inside a puffy cloud frame, colourable):
- `dreamSheaves`: one tall sheaf with eleven small sheaves bowing to it, over Joseph's Canaan mark (S02).
  `dreamSheaves2` is the same drawing over the Egypt side for S09; it shares `dreamSheaves`' sheet regions, so it
  is not printed twice.
- `dreamStars`: a smiling sun, a crescent moon and eleven stars in an arc.
- `dreamCows`: three fat cows and three skinny cows walking out of a wavy river line (seven would be too small to
  colour; the narrator says seven).
- `dreamGrain`: a stalk with fat ears and a stalk with thin ears.

## 5. Music (original score; warm cinematic orchestra, shepherd colours in Canaan, harp and sistrum in Egypt)

Two themes: the **Joseph theme** (a clear, hopeful tune on a shepherd's flute) and the **dream motif** (a rising
harp glissando and three bell notes that returns every time a dream cloud appears).

| Cue | Character |
|---|---|
| `home` | pastoral: flute, plucked strings, light frame drum; the Joseph theme |
| `dream` | the dream motif over a soft pad; playful under the brother's outburst |
| `betrayal` | low strings, a slow drum; the Joseph theme in minor on a reed; ends on an empty fifth |
| `prison` | very quiet; a single cello; the theme returns softly at "You are still with me" |
| `pharaoh` | pompous and a bit silly: a slow march with sistrum and low reeds, then the dream motif gone strange |
| `reveal` | calm, clear harp; wisdom |
| `plenty` | bright Egyptian celebration: harp, sistrum, hand drums; a short fanfare |
| `famine` | dry, sparse: woodblock, a lonely reed, wind |
| `brothers` | tense but warm; the dream motif answered by the Joseph theme |
| `iam` | the emotional peak: the theme held back, then released in full strings at "I am Joseph" |
| `reunion` | the theme as a slow, tearful-happy waltz for father and son |
| `finale` | full statement of the theme with the dream motif on top; ends on a held chord |

SFX: `sheep_baa sparkle harp_gliss camel_grunt coins cell_door moo fanfare wind_desert soft_chime` (CONTRACT §7).
