# Saul Sees the Light! — art direction (costume, make-up, props, set, light, music)

Same family as Ruth, Daniel, Jonah, Real Miracles, The King Who Serves and He Is Risen!: Paper & Crayon look, rounded
readable shapes, decal faces, same head-to-body ratio. **Every colourable region is a closed shape ≥ 6 mm on the A4
print.** Small parts (sandals, cords, eyes, the pouch strap, rope) are fixed-colour detail lines, not regions. All
designs are original; the reference viewing lent only the logic below (a young man in a dark red robe and a darker
travel cloak with a short dark beard; a sunset-coloured desert road with rocks and palms; a light from above that turns
everything white-blue; flat-roofed limestone houses on a narrow street; an older believer in a pale head shawl; a
courtyard of water for the baptism; a high city wall with a small window, torches by the gate at night; a lamp-lit room
in Jerusalem).

Period: about AD 34-36. Jerusalem; the desert road north; Damascus in Syria.

## 1. Characters

### Saul (`saul`) — height 0.37 (printed, centre; the hero)
- A young man of about thirty: a lean face, **thick dark eyebrows** (they carry the whole change of heart), a **short,
  neat dark-brown beard**, short dark hair. A **deep-red robe** to the shins, a **dark-blue travel cloak** over both
  shoulders and down the back (bone `cloak1`), a **wide brown leather belt** with a small pouch on the right hip
  (detail), sandals (detail).
- **One design for the whole story; only the face changes.** No costume change after he is changed (the change is in
  the face, the walk and the music).
- Props (hidden by default): `letters` (a rolled scroll tied with a cord, 0.06 long; child of `handR`; S01-S05), `bread`
  (a round flat loaf, 0.05; `handR`; S09), and the **basket rig** `basket` (a big round woven basket 0.13 across, 0.10
  high, with two ropes up from its rim; S11; CONTRACT §3).
- **Expressions**: `angry` (default in S01-S04: brows down hard, mouth a tight line), `shout` (brows down, mouth open
  square: "March!"), `shock` (eyes round, brows high), `look_up` (eyes up, mouth a small O, brows soft), `blind` (eyes
  gently closed, brows a little raised, a calm mouth: never screwed up, never bandaged), `pray` (eyes closed, a small
  peaceful mouth), `see` (eyes wide open and shining, a big open smile), `kind` (default from S08 on: brows relaxed and
  lifted, a warm closed smile), `talk`, `laugh`, `chew` (cheeks round, eyes smiling), `whisper` (lips rounded, eyes
  sparkling).
- **Regions**: skin, hair + beard, robe, cloak, belt.

### Ananias (`ananias`) — height 0.33 (printed, left)
- A small, round older man: a round face with **rosy cheeks**, a **grey curly beard**, **bushy grey eyebrows**, a bald
  top under a **small brown round cap**; a **moss-green robe** to the ankles, a **mustard shawl** over both shoulders,
  sandals (detail). Clearly unlike Saul (round, grey, green) and Barnabas (big, black-bearded, orange).
- Prop: `jug` (a terracotta water jug with a handle, 0.06 high; child of `handR`; S09).
- **Expressions**: `gentle` (default), `talk`, `look_up` (rapt, in the light), `fright` (eyes huge, mouth a wobbly
  zigzag, brows right up: "THAT Saul?!"), `gulp` (eyes squeezed, cheeks puffed), `brave` (chin up, a small determined
  smile), `joy`, `whisper`.
- **Regions**: skin, beard, cap, robe, shawl.

### Barnabas (`barnabas`) — height 0.39 (printed, right)
- A big, broad, warm man: **black curly hair and a full black beard**, a wide smile, big hands; a **terracotta-orange
  robe**, a **cream mantle** over the left shoulder, a **dark-green sash**, sandals (detail).
- **Expressions**: `happy` (default), `talk`, `laugh`, `kind`.
- **Regions**: skin, hair + beard, robe, mantle, sash.

### Judas (`judas`) — height 0.35 (fixed paint)
- A homely, kind householder: a short brown beard, a **striped blue-and-white head cloth**, a **sand tunic**, a
  **brown apron-sash**. Prop: `bread` (the same loaf, `handR`).
- **Expressions**: the Miracles friends' group drawings (`happy`, `talk`, `amazed`, `laugh`).

### The high priest (`priest`) — height 0.36 (fixed paint; one shot)
- An old man with a long white beard, calm and grave, **not a cartoon villain** (no sneer, no claws, no dark shadows on
  the face): a **white robe**, a **blue over-robe** with a **gold** hem band, a white cloth turban. No breastplate gems
  (too small to read; and not needed). Prop: a scroll is handed over (it is Saul's `letters`; the priest's hand is
  empty after S01).
- **Expressions**: `grave` (default: eyes calm, mouth straight), `talk`.

### The two temple guards (`tallguard`, `shortguard`) — heights 0.41 / 0.30 (fixed paint; a comic pair)
- **Tall guard**: long and thin, a long neck, a long nose, a thin moustache, a **tall pointed cloth cap**, a **grey-blue
  tunic** with a **rust** sash. Prop: `lantern` (a small clay lantern on a cord, `handR`; S11).
- **Short guard**: short and round, a big round face, a bushy black beard, a **flat round cap**, the same grey-blue
  tunic with a rust sash (they are a pair), a big belly.
- **No weapons at all** (no spears, no swords, no clubs): they carry Saul's travel bags (detail shapes on the back) and,
  at night, a lantern.
- **Expressions**: the Miracles friends' group drawings (`happy`, `talk`, `amazed`, `shock`) plus new `puff` (red
  cheeks, mouth open panting), `sleepy` (eyes half-closed, a smile), `asleep` (eyes shut, mouth open), `puzzled` (one
  brow up, mouth to one side).

### Peter (`peter`) — height 0.38 (Real Miracles design, fixed paint here)
- Exactly the Miracles Peter (`docs/miracles/DESIGN.md` §1, §3), painted with the Miracles demo colours into
  `extras_paint.png`, as in He Is Risen!.
- **Expressions**: Miracles `jolly`, `talk`, `shock`, `laugh`; He Is Risen! `sad`; new `afraid` (eyes round, brows up,
  mouth small and wobbly).

### Jesus — no model
Jesus is **not shown in body**: on the road He is a column of warm white-gold light from above (`road_light`), in
Ananias's house a soft column (`angel_light`). No silhouette, no face, no figure in the light.

## 2. Set: `damascus` (one page, three places)

Left = Damascus inside its wall. Centre = the city wall, seen edge-on from the front. Right = the desert road and
Jerusalem. **No weapons, no prison, no chains, no crosses anywhere on the page.**

- **City wall** (`cityWall`): a tall wall of **sand-coloured blocks** (drawn block lines), 0.06 thick, running from the
  back edge to near the front at x = 0, top 0.30 with rounded battlements, ending at the front in a round **tower**
  (top 0.34). The **gate** (`cityGate`): a round-topped arch through the wall (0.12 wide, 0.24 high) with two open
  wooden doors folded back inside. Two **torches** in iron holders (`torch1`, `torch2`) on the outside face either side
  of the gate. High in the outside face, further back, a small **window** (`wallWindow`, 0.07 wide) with a wooden
  shutter: the basket window. (Judas's house is built against the inside of the wall; the window belongs to it.)
- **Inside the city** (left half), along **Straight Street** (`straightStreet`: a pale paved street with drawn stones,
  from the gate to the left edge):
  - **Ananias's house** (`ananiasHouse`, far left back): a small flat-roofed limestone room, **open at the front like a
    doll's house**: a low stool, a lamp on a ledge, a rolled mat, a window in the back wall.
  - **The synagogue** (`synagogue`, middle back): a facade with two round columns, a lintel with a simple carved pattern
    (no letters), a wooden door, and **three wide steps** in front (top step y 0.04).
  - **Judas's house** (`judasHouse`, right back, against the wall): a bigger flat-roofed room, **open at the front**
    with a low sill and a doorway gap; inside a **prayer mat**, a **bread basket** (`breadBasket`), a low table, a big
    **water jar** (`waterJar`), a window high in the back-right corner (into the city wall).
  - Front-left: a **well** (`well`, h ≤ 0.06) with a bucket, two pots, a low market crate.
- **Outside the city** (right half):
  - **The road** (`road`): pale sand-gold packed earth from the high priest's porch (right back) down the right side,
    along the front, to the gate; **desert rocks** (`rocks`, low), a few low **bushes**, a **milestone** (`milestone`,
    h ≤ 0.08), one **palm** (`palm`) on the right edge (crown high, underside ≥ 0.30).
  - **Jerusalem** (right back): the **high priest's porch** (`priestPorch`): a raised porch with four columns, a flat
    roof, two steps; behind it a stretch of **Jerusalem's wall** (`jerusalemWall`, back edge, low relief). Beside it the
    **believers' house** (`jerusalemHouse`), a small limestone house with a door (`jerusalemDoor`) and a window.
- Far back left: low hills and a few distant flat roofs of Damascus (low relief).

## 5. Music (original score; the warm acoustic band of the Jesus packs: oud, flute, harp, strings, frame drum, a
children's "ah" choir; for Saul a low brass and snare colour)

Two themes:
- **Saul's march**: a stomping, minor 4-bar march (low strings, tuba, snare, a stern oud figure): bossy, comic rather
  than menacing. It is the "turned around" theme: from S08 it returns **in major, at a skipping pace, on flute and
  pizzicato**, and in the finale it is played by the whole band in major with the choir: the same tune, a changed heart.
- **The Jesus theme**: reused unchanged from `tools/story/music_miracles.py` (`jesus()`, the calm rising melody in D
  major) for the light and voice (S03-S04, S07) and for the healing (S08). The finale joins Saul's major march with the
  Jesus theme's first bars (the `JOINT` harmony idea in `music_miracles.py`).

| Cue | Character |
|---|---|
| `angry_march` | Saul's march in minor: low strings and tuba, a stern snare; comic-stern, not scary |
| `road` | the march at a hot, trudging pace; a puffing bassoon for the guards; heat shimmer on a high violin |
| `light` | the march breaks off; a swelling shimmering string chord, harp glissando and choir "ah" (no thunder); the Jesus theme on flute, slow and warm, under the voice |
| `dark_walk` | slow, quiet: solo cello, the march figure fragmented and gentle, footsteps in a soft drum |
| `three_days` | very soft harp and pad, a slow prayerful flute; the Jesus theme's first bar, unresolved |
| `ananias` | a nervous comic tiptoe (bassoon and pizzicato), the Jesus theme answering it warmly each time the voice speaks; a "gulp" on a woodblock |
| `brother` | tiptoe pizzicato, then the Jesus theme in full on strings at "Brother Saul"; a bright harp at the scales; the first hint of the march in major |
| `baptism` | fresh and happy: harp and flute, a splash of glockenspiel, a chuckle on the oud for the bread |
| `new_saul` | the march in major for the first time, skipping, on flute and oud, hand drum |
| `basket` | night tiptoe: pizzicato and muted horn, a creaky slow descent (a falling bassoon line), a snore on the tuba |
| `barnabas` | warm, rocking 6/8: oud and strings; a little "worried" clarinet for Peter that turns to joy at the welcome |
| `finale` | Saul's march in major with the whole band and choir, joined by the Jesus theme; hand claps; a bright held chord |
