# David & Goliath — music and sound effects

Generated by `tools/make_dg_music.py` from `assets/david-goliath/timeline.json`. Do not hand-edit the audio files;
change the script and rerun it (about 20 s):

```
python3 tools/make_dg_music.py            # music + sfx + preview
python3 tools/make_dg_music.py --skip-music --skip-sfx   # preview only (e.g. after new voice clips)
```

Needs `mido numpy scipy`, `fluid-synth`, `ffmpeg`, and macOS's General MIDI bank
`/System/Library/Components/CoreAudio.component/Contents/Resources/gs_instruments.dls` (same as `tools/make_music.py`).
The script asserts that every `music.cue` and `sfx.id` in the timeline has a composer/synth, so a new id in the timeline
fails loudly instead of shipping silence. The random seed is fixed, so a full rerun gives the same files.

## Formats (CONTRACT section 7)

- `assets/david-goliath/audio/music/<cue>.wav` and `audio/sfx/<id>.wav`: 44.1 kHz, mono, 16-bit PCM.
- `.mp3` copy next to every WAV: 96 kbps mono (MP3 encoder padding adds ~0.04 s; use the WAV lengths for timing).
- Music: −20 LUFS integrated (EBU R128, measured by ffmpeg `ebur128`, two-pass gain). True peak kept ≤ −1 dBTP.
- SFX: sample peak −3.0 dBFS.
- Machine-readable manifests: `audio/music/cues.json` (lengths, loudness, crossfade rule) and `audio/sfx/mix.json`
  (lengths, peaks, `loop`, recommended `gainDb`).

## Music cues

How to play them: start each cue at its timeline `t` at full level and fade the previous cue out over 0.6 s from that
`t`. Every cue except the last is its span + 0.6 s long, so the fade-out has real music under it. `sting_hit` is a
one-shot and rings out by itself; there is deliberate silence from its end until `theme_victory` (76.0 s). Duck music
about 6 dB under voice lines (the preview does this).

impacts, rising-sine bubbles for water); `victory_chime` is a celesta/glockenspiel/harp run rendered with FluidSynth.

## Preview mix

`assets/david-goliath/audio/preview_mix.mp3` (90.04 s, 96 kbps mono, −17.1 LUFS, −1.2 dBTP after a −1 dB limiter):
all cues with the 0.6 s crossfades, every timeline sfx at its `t` and `gainDb`, `sling_whoosh` looped and cut at the
release, plus the English voice clips from `audio/voice/en/` when present (18/18 were present at the last run) with
the music ducked 6 dB under them. It is a rough check of timing and balance, not a deliverable for the app.

## Known limits

- The animal, roar, laugh and crowd sounds are synthetic and read as cartoon-like; they were checked by measurement and
  spectrogram, not by ear. Replace any of them with a recording (same id, same format) if they sound wrong.
- Cue lengths are tied to the timeline spans. If Story moves a `music` entry, rerun the script.
