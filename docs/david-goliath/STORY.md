## Logline

A shepherd boy the size of a giant's knee walks out alone, with five smooth stones and his trust in God, and brings
down the champion no soldier dared to face.

一个还没巨人膝盖高的放羊少年，带着五块光滑的石头和对上帝的信靠，打倒了全军都不敢面对的巨人。

## Theme

Courage and trust matter more than size. David is not brave because he is strong; he is brave because he remembers
how God helped him before (the lion and the bear) and trusts Him now. The story says this twice, in David's own words
(dg_10, dg_14, dg_17), before the narrator says it once at the end (dg_18).

勇气和信靠比个子更重要。大卫不是因为力气大才勇敢，而是记得上帝以前怎样帮助他（狮子和熊），所以现在也信靠祂。

## Stage rules

- Only two actors are on the page: **David** (≈ 0.30 tall) and **Goliath** (≈ 0.80, about 2.7×). Saul, the two armies
  and the narrator are voices and sounds only. The armour, the lion and the bear are never modelled: a voice, an SFX
  and one memory-bubble effect carry them.
- Goliath holds the back of the page (z ≈ −0.25); David works the front, where the brook is. Goliath falls forward
  toward the viewer, which keeps him off David and gives the child holding the phone the "boom".
- Child-friendly cuts: Goliath's threat to feed David to the birds (17:44) becomes "Little boy, you want to
  fight me?" (17:42, he looked down on him for being young; the "sticks" of 17:43 are dropped because David has no
  staff in the design); David's taking of the sword and the head (17:50–51) is left out. The stone, the fall and the cheer are
  enough. No blood, no weapon hits anyone except the one stone.
- Height: the Masoretic text says six cubits and a span (about 2.9 m); some ancient manuscripts say four cubits and a
  span (about 2 m). We follow the familiar reading in kid terms: "almost three metres" / 「差不多有三米高」.
- God's name: 「耶和华」 / "the LORD" in David's key line (dg_14), as in the Chinese Union Version and most English
  Bibles; 「上帝」 elsewhere in Chinese because that is what children hear at church and at home.
