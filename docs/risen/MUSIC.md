# He Is Risen! — music and sound effects

Generated by `tools/story/music_risen.py` from the timeline (see "Which timeline"). Do not hand-edit the audio files;
change the script and rerun it:

```
python3 tools/story/music_risen.py                           # music + sfx + preview + the tables below
python3 tools/story/music_risen.py --skip-music --skip-sfx   # preview only (e.g. after new voice clips)
python3 tools/story/music_risen.py --only mary,thomas        # re-render some cues, keep the others' rows
python3 tools/story/music_risen.py --stems                   # also print per-stem levels (for balancing)
python3 tools/story/music_risen.py --spec /tmp/risen_spec    # also write a spectrogram PNG of every output
```

Same toolchain and shape as `music_lastsupper.py` (`mido numpy scipy`, `fluid-synth`, `ffmpeg`, macOS
`gs_instruments.dls`); imports `music_kit.py`, `music_polish.py` and `voice_margin.py` without changing them, and
imports `JESUS` / `JESUS_HARM` from `music_miracles.py`, so Real Miracles, The King Who Serves and this pack play one
Jesus theme (if that theme is ever edited, rerun all three scripts). A full run takes about one minute. Scratch:
`/tmp/risen_music/` (emptied per cue, removed at exit). Seeds are per cue / effect (`risen/<id>`), so reruns are
identical. Engine and licence: our own synthesis (FluidSynth + the macOS DLS bank, numpy DSP), as in every pack (R7).
The tables between the `<!-- …:start/end -->` markers are rewritten by the script.

## Listen first

Nobody has listened yet; everything below was judged by measurement (1 s RMS profiles, band energy, spectrograms,
loop checks) and the voice-over-bed engine model. Please listen in this order (times are the zh play in
`preview_mix.mp3`):

1. **`mary` (140 – 151 s)**, the emotional peak: one held A (strings) through Jesus' "Mary.", a harp sweep, then the
   morning theme on flute + violins; its high F# and the choir sit on the `sparkle_up` swell in the gap after "It's
   You!" (the loudest moment of this cue, −15 dB RMS). Judge whether the burst is tender enough, and whether the
   held A reads as a held breath or as silence (the first second is the crossfade from `garden_tears`, −39 dB).
2. **`sad_day` (0 – 25 s)**: must be sad but never dark or frightening; D minor strings + harp, no drums, then the
   PROMISE figure on solo flute left hanging on Asus. Check that it is gentle enough for a 4-year-old.
3. **`stone` (42 – 57 s)**: soft timpani roll under `rumble`, one soft stroke on `stone_roll`, then the shimmer and
   harp glissando for the light. The hit second is −12 dB RMS, the loudest second of the score (bright, but check
   it is not scary with the `rumble` + `stone_roll` effects on top).
4. **`race` (88 – 102 s)**: pizz scamper (John) vs tuba oom-pah (Peter); under "Wait... wait for me!" the tuba drags
   and sighs, and the slide whistle plays in the longest pause inside the zh clip (96.45 – 97.01 s). In English the
   pause falls elsewhere, so the whistle may land on a word.
5. **`finale` (208 – 226 s)**: the whole JESUS theme (8 bars) as the Easter-morning tutti; it sits under the narrator
   all the way and only swells on the last chord. Check it sounds joyful rather than polite.
6. **`thomas`**: doubt clarinet (PROMISE figure in B minor, twice) → slow morning theme whose D lands on "My Lord and
   my God!". Dipped 3 dB under both Thomas lines.
7. **`peace`**: the ghost tiptoe for "A g-g-ghost?" must be comic, not spooky; the fish wood blocks + `munch`.
8. **SFX**: `birds_dawn`, `run_steps` (two runners, the second heavier), `munch`, `rumble` (soft, never a crash).
9. `preview_mix.mp3` start to end for balance.

## Which timeline

Cues are **composed for the play as the phone runs it in Chinese** (`voice_margin.lang_timeline("risen", "zh")`,
225.9 s at the last run). It is the shortest play, so no cadence is cut off by the next cue. In-cue hits are looked
up by line id and event. Each file is long enough for its **longest** section across the zh play, the en play
(232.4 s) and `assets/risen/timeline.json` (247.4 s) × 1.3 + 0.6 s (`longestSpan` in `cues.json`); past its cadence
each cue continues into a quiet loop section on its final chord (`[loopStart, loopEnd)`, sample-exact, identical
tiles). **Rerun this script after any re-time.** Last checked against the timeline written at 05:07:45 on
2026-11-18 (cue starts, spans, line starts and every hit identical to what was rendered).

## Formats (CONTRACT section 7)

- `assets/risen/audio/music/<cue>.wav` and `audio/sfx/<id>.wav`: 44.1 kHz, mono, 16-bit PCM, plus a 96 kbps mono
  `.mp3` next to each.
- Music: the composed span at −20.5 LUFS, with a little range inside the contract's −20 ±1: `sad_day` and
  `garden_tears` −21.0, `empty` −20.9, `mary` and `finale` −20.0, `race` −19.8. Loop beds 4.5 dB under their cue.
  True peak ≤ −1 dBTP. Below 45 Hz removed; the speech pocket (−1.5 dB at 900 Hz, −3.5 dB at 2.4 kHz); the score sits
  7 dB down under the story and swells on `stone_roll` and on the first `sparkle_up` (both clear of speech in zh) and
  on the finale's last chord (`music_polish.underscore`). `DIPS` (by line id, resolved on every run): rs_15 −2 dB
  (news), rs_21 −3 dB (empty), rs_35 and rs_37 −3 dB (thomas).
- Phone speakers: the weight is in the mids; energy below 150 Hz is 1-21 % of each cue (race and peace highest),
  0.15-2 kHz carries 79-98 %.
- SFX: sample peak −3.0 dBFS; all **one-shots** sized to their scene (StoryDirector stops loops only at a music cue);
  `gainDb` in `mix.json` is the recommended playback gain.
- `assets/risen/audio/preview_mix.mp3`: the zh play (39/39 lines) with music and SFX as `StoryDirector` mixes them.

## The themes

One melody, two faces, both from the Miracles `JESUS` theme (D major: A-B-D / E…D / F#-E-D-B / A-B-D …):

- **PROMISE figure** (`promise()`): the theme's first four notes, A-B-D-E, stopping on E (unanswered). `sad_day`
  (solo flute, slow), `dawn` (a hint in G: D-E-G-A), `garden_tears` (cello, B minor with a minor 6th: F#-G-B-C#),
  `thomas` (clarinet, B minor, twice), `empty` (answered at last: E → D on "and He did!"), `news` (fast flute runs).
- **MORNING theme**: the JESUS theme itself. `risen` (bars 1-4, violins + choir, first time, glowing),
  `mary` (bursting out on flute + violins), `thomas` (slow, violins + horn), `finale` (all 8 bars, the whole band).

## Weak or unverified

- **Not listened to by anyone.** All judgements are measurements and spectrograms.
- English play: hits drift up to 2.4 s (table above); the `mary` swell lands 1.8 s late in English, possibly under
  "Go and tell My friends", and the slide whistle position comes from the zh clip's pause only.
- `stone` hit second (−12 dB RMS) plus `rumble`/`stone_roll` on top: measured loud-ish for this score; meant as
  bright wonder, but it is the likeliest place a small child hears "big noise".
- `mary` opens nearly silent for ~1 s (crossfade from `garden_tears`, then the held A at −26 dB): intended as a held
  breath before "Mary.", could read as a drop-out.
- `garden_tears` tail (under "Sir, are you the gardener?") is very quiet (−26 dB RMS) on purpose.
- The SFX are synthesised: whether the birds read as birds, the footsteps as two people running, and `munch` as a
  crunchy bite (not a crackle) needs ears.
- `finale` stays under the narrator (−25 dB RMS) for most of its length; the "joyful tutti" is only fully heard on
  the last chord and in the loop section after it.
