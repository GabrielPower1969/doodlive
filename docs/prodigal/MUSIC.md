# The prodigal son — music and sound effects

Generated by `tools/story/music_prodigal.py` from `assets/prodigal/timeline.json`. Do not hand-edit the audio files;
change the script and rerun it:

```
python3 tools/story/music_prodigal.py                          # music + sfx + preview + the tables below
python3 tools/story/music_prodigal.py --skip-music --skip-sfx  # preview only (e.g. after new voice clips)
python3 tools/story/music_prodigal.py --only run,brother       # re-render some cues, keep the others' manifest rows
python3 tools/story/music_prodigal.py --check --png /tmp/x.png # measure the written files (+ a spectrogram per cue)
```

Same toolchain as Exodus and Ruth (`mido numpy scipy`, `fluid-synth`, `ffmpeg`, macOS's `gs_instruments.dls`); a full
run takes about 2 minutes. The script **imports `music_esther.py` and `music_ruth.py` as libraries** (Score / Part /
mel / groove, the renderer, the hall, loudness and file helpers, the synthesis primitives, Esther's wah-wah trombone,
Ruth's split render for loop sections and her bird and cricket synths) and changes neither, so Esther's, Ruth's and
Exodus's output is untouched. All music is original and synthesised from MIDI through the GM bank plus a few
synthesised sounds mixed into cues (pig snorts, the wah-wah); no samples from any film or recording. The tables
between the `<!-- …:start/end -->` markers are rewritten by the script.

**Rerun this script whenever `build_timeline.py prodigal` re-times the play** (new voice clips): every in-cue hit is
looked up by event (see "Timeline hooks"); the script asserts every `music.cue` and `sfx.id` has a composer/synth and
every cue file is at least span × 1.3 + 0.6 s. Seeds are per cue / per effect (`prodigal/<id>`), so reruns are
byte-identical and changing one cue never changes another.

## Surviving a stretched section (re-timing at load)

Exactly as Ruth and Exodus: each cue's composed music ends on its cadence at `loopFromSec` (= its span) and continues
into a quiet bed on its final chord, ~3 dB under the cue. `[loopStart, loopEnd)` (seconds, plus exact
`loopStartSample` / `loopEndSample`) is one repeat of that bed, whole bars, at least 4 s, built as sample-identical
tiles, so jumping from loopEnd to loopStart is the same signal as playing straight on (checked below). Without
looping, every file runs at least 30 % past its span and fades out over its last 2.5 s.

Loop beds: `home` harp bed on Dm (the father's sadness); `leaving` oud walk on D; `party` / `feast` the hand-drum
dance on D; `broke` a thin tremolo + low drone on Dm; `pigs` a soft oom-pah on G; `homesick`, `run`, `brother` harp
bed on D; `road_home` tremolo + soft timpani on A (the dominant, still waiting for the run); `finale` celesta bells.

`crickets_night` is a whole-file seamless 20 s loop. **The timeline has no `crickets_night` event yet**, so
`mix.json` gives `startAtCue: "feast"` (S11, the night) and `stopAtCue: "finale"`, plus `t` (the feast cue's current
time) and `inTimeline: false`. Either the director starts the bed when cue `feast` starts, or Story adds an sfx event
`crickets_night` at the start of S11 and this script picks it up (`inTimeline` turns true).

## Formats (CONTRACT section 7)

- `assets/prodigal/audio/music/<cue>.wav` and `audio/sfx/<id>.wav`: 44.1 kHz, mono, 16-bit PCM; a 96 kbps mono
  `.mp3` next to each (MP3 padding adds ~0.03 s; use the WAV lengths for timing).
- Music: −20.0 LUFS integrated over the span (EBU R128 via ffmpeg `ebur128`), true peak ≤ −1 dBTP.
- SFX: sample peak −3.0 dBFS. `gainDb` in `mix.json` is the recommended playback gain on top of that.

## The father's theme

One original 8-bar melody (`FATHER` in the script), D major. Its head is six notes, **F♯–G–A–D | B–A**: a step up,
an open-armed leap to the top, a settling fall. In D minor (F♯ → F, B → B♭) it is the same shape, sad.

| Where | How |
|---|---|
| `home` (S01, the narrator) | ney-flute, bars 1–4, over oud plucks and harp |
| `home` (S02, the father gives the money) | the head in **D minor** on the cello, slowly, over Gm–Dm |
| `leaving` (S03, the father waves) | the head at half speed, faint, on slow strings under the son's whistle |
| `homesick` (S07) | a single ney-flute plays the head in **D minor**; lifts to D major on "I'll go home!" |
| `road_home` (S09, "every day the father looked down the road") | the head on the cello, tender |
| `run` (S09–S10) | **the head bursts out on the full strings + flute + soft horns as he runs; its high B lands on the hug** as a huge G chord settling onto a held D major; then bars 5–8 dance under "Quick! The best robe!" |
| `feast` (S11) | the head in dancing eighths is the feast tune |
| `brother` (S13) | the father's gentle answer: bars 1–4 + 7 on a soft clarinet, cadencing on D as the brother takes his hand |
| `finale` (S14) | all eight bars, warm and full from the golden rays, held D major chord |

## Exodus feedback applied

- No GM shakuhachi: the ney is a low-passed GM flute with delayed vibrato plus a breath of recorder (`ney()`).
- No trumpets and no "aah" choir anywhere (they read as an old video game). The big moments (`run`, `finale`) are
  strings (violins, violas, cellos, contrabass), harp, timpani, frame drum and horns kept soft and low-passed
  (≤ 2.4 kHz) as a warm pad, with at most a very faint "ooh" under the hug chord and the finale.

## Known limits

<!-- limits:start -->
- Checked by measurement only (loudness, seams, spectra, level scans, spectrograms), **not by ear**. Listen first
  to: **`run`** (the heart: GM strings + soft horns; if the burst is thin, raise `vln`/`hn`, not the automation),
  the **ney** (GM flute + recorder breath; it should read as a breathy reed flute, not a concert flute; if not,
  lower `ney()`'s `lp` to ~3500), and the **fiddle** in `party` (GM fiddle 110 may sound country rather than
  Hellenistic; GM violin 40 is the fallback).
- **Weakest cues:** `party` (GM fiddle + Hijaz runs at 134 bpm; "slightly too fast" comedy depends on the fiddle
  sample), `leaving` (GM whistle; with the voices re-timed its span is only 9 s, so it runs at 133 bpm, perkier than
  planned), `broke` (Esther's wah-wah reused; it ends on F against the famine's D minor, by design).
- **Weakest effects:** `oink` and the snorts in `pigs` (formant pig voice; may read as a squeaky toy or a goose),
  `cheer` (synthetic crowd, the weakest link in every pack; here 22 voices for 3–4 friends), `coins` (one file for
  both the purse hand-over at S02 and the coin fountain at S04: the first 0.5 s is the bag's jingle, then coins land
  and bounce; if the hand-over sounds wrong, ask Story for a separate `coins_bag` id), `tummy` (a low growl + bubbles;
  phone speakers lose most of it below 150 Hz, so its gainDb is high at −6).
- **Duplicated hits are deliberate but should be balanced in the app:** `run` timpani + cymbal on the father's first
  step with `footsteps_run`; the `run` celesta with `sparkle` at S10; the `party` xylophone run with `coins`; the
  snorts inside `pigs` with the `oink` sfx.
- `crickets_night` has **no timeline event**: start it at cue `feast` (`startAtCue`) until Story adds one.
- Lengths follow a timeline re-timed from 26/29 voice durations (13/29 zh clips on disk at the last preview): rerun
  `build_timeline.py prodigal` and then this script when the rest land (about 1 minute).
