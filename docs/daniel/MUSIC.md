# Daniel — music and sound effects

Generated by `tools/story/music_daniel.py` from `assets/daniel/timeline.json`. Do not hand-edit the audio files;
change the script and rerun it:

```
python3 tools/story/music_daniel.py                          # music + sfx + preview + the tables below
python3 tools/story/music_daniel.py --skip-music --skip-sfx  # preview only (e.g. after new voice clips)
python3 tools/story/music_daniel.py --only den,angel         # re-render some cues, keep the others' manifest rows
python3 tools/story/music_daniel.py --stems                  # also print per-stem levels (for balancing)
```

Same toolchain as Esther and Ruth (`mido numpy scipy`, `fluid-synth`, `ffmpeg`, macOS's `gs_instruments.dls`). A full
run takes about 3 minutes. The script **imports `tools/story/music_esther.py` as a library** and does not change it;
Ruth's loop-section machinery (vamp + sample-identical loop tiles, the sustain fix) is copied in from
`music_ruth.py` and generalised (more vamp styles, a cue that plays twice, pitch-bend parts). Scratch files go to
`/tmp/daniel_music/` (`--tmp`), emptied after every cue and removed at exit (the Ruth run once filled the disk).
Seeds are per cue / per effect (`daniel/<id>`), so reruns are reproducible and changing one cue never changes
another. The tables between the `<!-- …:start/end -->` markers are rewritten by the script.

**The timeline is still an estimate (0/29 voiced). When Voice lands and `build_timeline.py daniel` re-times it,
rerun this script** so the in-cue hits follow the new times. Every in-cue hit is looked up by event (see "Timeline
hooks"); the script asserts that every `music.cue` and `sfx.id` has a composer / synth and that every cue file is
at least span × 1.3 + 0.6 s.

## Surviving a stretched section (re-timing at load)

As Ruth (see `docs/ruth/MUSIC.md`): every cue's composed music ends on its cadence at `loopFromSec` (= the span it
was composed for) and continues into a quiet bed on its final chord, ~3 dB under the cue. `[loopStart, loopEnd)`
(seconds, plus exact `loopStartSample` / `loopEndSample`) is one repeat of that bed, whole bars and at least 4 s;
the repeats are **sample-identical tiles**, so a jump from loopEnd to loopStart is the same signal as playing
straight on. Without looping, just keep playing: every file runs at least 30 % past its span and fades over its
last 2.5 s. Bed styles per cue: oud + frame-drum pluck (`babylon`, `dawn_run`), harp arpeggios (`king_friend`,
`decree`, `prayer`), pizzicato tiptoe + wood blocks (`scheme`), soft heartbeat + low pad (`trouble`, `den`), harp +
oohs (`angel`), the clock still ticking (`sleepless`), harp + celesta bell (`rescue`, `finale`).

**`scheme` plays twice** (S03 at 33.58 and S06 at 97.85). One file, composed for the longer S03 section (its
events drive the in-cue hits; `composedFor` / `composedSpan` in `cues.json`); at S06 it plays from its start
(the sulky tiptoe tune fits the official sneaking behind the palm) and is crossfaded into `trouble` after
~10.7 s, just as the S03 "idea" stop-and-zip arrives (10.5 s into the file). `cues.json` has one row per
occurrence.

Ambience: `crickets_night` is a **whole-file seamless loop** (`loop: true`, loopStart 0, loopEnd = length, wrap
crossfaded with an equal-power 0.6 s fold), `stopAtCue: sleepless`, `fadeInSec` 1.0, `fadeOutSec` 1.5.
**Integration:** stop it when `sleepless` starts (Ruth note: the director's single `sfxLoop` source otherwise only
stops at the story end); the preview mix plays it this way.

## Formats (CONTRACT section 7)

- `assets/daniel/audio/music/<cue>.wav` and `audio/sfx/<id>.wav`: 44.1 kHz, mono, 16-bit PCM; a 96 kbps mono `.mp3`
  next to each (MP3 padding adds ~0.03 s; use the WAV lengths for timing).
- Music: −20.0 LUFS integrated over the composed span (EBU R128 via ffmpeg `ebur128`), loop bed −23, true peak
  ≤ −1 dBTP.
- SFX: sample peak −3.0 dBFS. `gainDb` in `mix.json` is the recommended playback gain on top of that.

## The Daniel theme

One original melody (`DANIEL_THEME` in the script). Bar 1 is the motif the design asks for: a calm, rising
five-note prayer figure, scale degrees 1-2-4-5-6 (E F# A B C in E minor, C# in E major); bar 3 repeats the shape a
fourth higher; bar 5 is home (E). Written in E minor; the major form raises G, C and D.

| Where | How |
|---|---|
| `babylon` (S01, the city) | introduced in **E minor on the duduk-like reed** (bars 1, 2, 4, 5) over oud, frame drum and strings |
| `king_friend` (Daniel: "my wisdom comes from God") | bars 1-2 on flute in **G major**, a hint |
| `prayer` (S05) | solo flute, **E minor** bars 1-2 during the narration, then **E major** bars 1, 2, 4, 5 when he kneels: the same words in a new light |
| `trouble` (the king: "how foolish") | the motif on cellos in E minor, slow and sorry |
| `den` ("God is with me") | the motif on the reed, calm over the tension, resolving to an **E major glimmer** |
| `angel` | **E major, glowing**: flute an octave up over violins, choir + oohs, harp, celesta (bars 1-5 as they fit) |
| `dawn_run` (Daniel: "I'm fine!") | bars 1-2 broadened (half speed) on **horns + flute** over the running strings |
| `rescue` | **triumphant**: violins, flute, horns, timpani, the full ensemble (bars 1, 4, 5 before the official sneaks off) |
| `finale` | **full warm statement** (bars 1-5): cello/viola/qanun under the proclamation, violins/flute/horns/oohs from the golden rays, held E major chord |
| sfx `fanfare` | the motif's head E-F#-A-B on trumpets, into a held E major chord |

## How the score is made

- As Ruth: composed as MIDI, one stem per instrument, rendered dry by FluidSynth, mixed in numpy with per-stem
  gain / filters / reverb send into one shared synthetic hall; mono; tempo stretched a few percent so whole bars
  fill each span; humanised onsets and velocities, delayed vibrato, expression swells; winds, bowed strings,
  voices **and brass** get Ruth's NRPN sustain fix so long notes hold.
- **Middle-Eastern colour without pastiche:** the GM bank has no duduk, ney, oud, qanun or frame drum, so (as Esther
  and Ruth) English horn + a breath of clarinet (duduk-like reed), nylon guitar (oud), koto (qanun), congas / bongos
  (frame drum / darbuka). The E hijaz scale (E F G# A B C D) colours `babylon` (harp and qanun runs, the low brass
  E-F-E), `scheme` (the sulky E-F half step) and `den` / `trouble` (E-F rocking); the theme itself is plain E minor /
  E major so it can glow.
- **Comedy is written in, not hoped for:** pitch-bend parts (`BPart`, ±12 semitone range) give the trombone 'pfff'
  and pompous sag, the slide-whistle zip on the idea, the droopy clarinet sighs and the big trombone 'waaah' in
  `sleepless`, the bassoon 'ho-hum'; plus hard stops before punchlines, 'dun-DUN!', the decree's 'ta-DAH!' on the
  stamp, a ticking clock that never stops, the xylophone 'boing' on the jump and the scurry fading off the page.
- **Den without horror:** consonant minor chords only (Em, F/E, G), no clusters, no screeching high strings; the
  build peaks around −17 LUFS short-term, then stops dead for the roar.

## Known limits

- Checked by measurement (ffmpeg ebur128, loop-seam analysis, spectra, spectrograms), **not by ear** (written
  unattended).
- Lengths are estimates until the voices land: rerun after the re-time (the loop / tail only covers the stretch;
  the in-cue hits stay where the old timeline put them until the rerun). Replace any cue or effect with a
  recording later: same id, same format, same loop fields.
- The `crickets_night` loop repeats every 20 s; the two palm rustles give it away if a night scene runs long.
