# Daniel — production contract (story pack #4)

The brief every role works from. Same rules as `docs/ruth/CONTRACT.md` (and through it Esther and David & Goliath)
unless this file says otherwise: units, UV = page position, audio formats, "only Integration runs Unity", nobody
commits, nobody touches another role's files. Story and lines: `docs/daniel/STORY.md` + `docs/daniel/storyboard/story.py`;
look: `docs/daniel/DESIGN.md`; architecture: `docs/STORY-ARCHITECTURE.md`, `docs/STORY-ENGINE.md`.

**Ruth (pack #3) is the template** and Esther (pack #2) the fallback: every `pipeline/ru_*.py`, `pipeline/es_*.py`,
`pipeline/story_mesh.py`, `tools/story/*.py`, `StoryFxRuth*.cs` / `StoryFxEsther*.cs` and `docs/ruth/*.md` is a
working, device-tested example. Start from a copy, keep what works, generalise shared helpers only when cheap and
without changing Esther's or Ruth's output. Read `docs/LESSONS.md` first (L44 onward especially).

Quality bar: at least Ruth's. New in this pack and the riskiest part: **two quadruped lions** (rig, skinning,
animation, faces). Lions must be lovable: soft round shapes, big mane tufts, expressive eyes and brows.

Disk: the Mac has about 25 GB free. Put scratch work in `/tmp/daniel_<role>/` and delete it when done; never leave
renderer or TTS temp files behind (the Ruth run filled the disk once).

## 3. Skeletons

Humans (`daniel king officer`): bones exactly as Ruth (`root hips spine chest neck head jaw shoulderL/R upperArmL/R
forearmL/R handL/R thighL/R shinL/R footL/R toeL/R fingersL fingersR eyelids`), A-pose, facing +Z. Extras:
- `daniel`: `hair1` (ponytail, from `head`), `sash1 sash2` (hanging sash end, from `hips`).
- `king`: `cape1 cape2` (from `chest`, the cape's back drape), `belly` (from `spine`, a jiggle bone for laughs and the
  joy jump), `ring` (child of `handR`, the signet ring; visible all play).
- `officer`: `robe1` (from `hips`, the robe's front hem swing), `scroll` (child of `handR`, the law; hidden by default).

Lions (`lion lion2`), facing +Z (nose toward the viewer), standing on all fours with paws on the page:
`root hips spine chest neck head jaw eyelids tail1 tail2 tail3 tail4 earL earR
 upperArmL forearmL pawL  upperArmR forearmR pawR   (front legs, from chest)
 thighL shinL footL  thighR shinR footR             (back legs, from hips)`
plus `mane` (from `neck`, a soft jiggle for the mane on roars and shakes; `lion` only, `lion2` keeps the bone with no
weights). `root` at the ground under the belly centre.

Props are rigid meshes parented to their bone; the director shows / hides them by `prop` events (`"officer/scroll"`):
the node name after the slash is the bone name **and** the mesh object (`officer_scroll` under bone `scroll`; Esther's
`king/ring` precedent).

Faces: decal textures on UV1 as Ruth, one PNG per expression in `docs/daniel/design/faces/<char>_<expr>.png`, plus a
blink by `eyelids`; lions too (eyes, brows, nose and mouth decal on the muzzle; the roar opens `jaw`). Expressions:
DESIGN.md.

## 7. Audio

Voice −16 LUFS for TTS / −17 LUFS for recordings, music −20 LUFS, SFX peak −3 dBFS, 44.1 kHz mono WAV + 96 kbps MP3,
as Ruth. Lines are public paraphrases of Scripture, so cloud TTS is allowed; nothing private goes into a request.

Voice brief: people telling a story to a child, not text being read. Ruth's method (Qwen3-TTS best-of-N with acting
notes, VoiceDesign for voices no stock voice has, real breaths, measured selection) is the floor. Distinct timbres:
Daniel calm and warm, not the same voice as Boaz or the Esther king; King Darius big and round, comic when fretting;
the official nasal and whiny, **not** Haman's `dylan` voice; his last line is a cartoon shriek. Interjections
(哼, 嘿嘿, 唉, 哇, 呃) must sound like sounds.

Music cues (ids): `babylon king_friend scheme decree prayer trouble den angel sleepless dawn_run rescue finale`
(`cues.json` with loop points as Ruth). SFX ids: `idea_ding seal_stamp roar growl stone_roll crickets_night
angel_choir yawn_lion purr rooster sparkle fanfare` (`mix.json`). The roar: big but warm and short (≤ 1.6 s), no
horror; the purr: a deep, funny motor rumble.
