# Esther — production contract (story pack #2)

The brief every role works from. Same rules as `docs/david-goliath/CONTRACT.md` unless this file says otherwise
(units, UV = page position, audio formats, "only Integration runs Unity", nobody commits). Story and lines:
`docs/esther/STORY.md`; look: `docs/esther/DESIGN.md`; architecture: `docs/STORY-ARCHITECTURE.md`.

Quality bar (owner review, 2026-11-13): David & Goliath's models were "average", its lines stiff and its voices sounded
like AI. This pack must look finer (faces, cloth, ornaments, light) and sound like people telling a story.

## 2. Characters (ids are the contract)

Units: art-width units (1.0 = art rectangle width), Y up, page = XZ plane at y = 0, +Z toward the viewer, +X right.

| Id | Height | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| `esther` | 0.34 | the hero; long braid, cape, diadem |
| `king` | 0.40 standing (sits most of the play) | sits on the throne; base height = dais top (`set.daisTop`, design.json) |
| `haman` | 0.37 | sly, comic when found out |

Sheet layout (rest pose, front orthographic, no overlaps): the Set + sheet role places the three figures and the set
pieces on the art rectangle and writes `sheetX/sheetY` per figure into `assets/esther/design.json`, exactly like
David & Goliath (`design.json` `characters.<id>.sheetX/sheetY`, `desert.pieces[].frontUV`).

## 3. Skeleton

Humanoid bone names exactly as David & Goliath (`root hips spine chest neck head jaw shoulderL/R upperArmL/R
forearmL/R handL/R thighL/R shinL/R footL/R toeL/R`), A-pose, facing +Z, plus finger curl bones `fingersL fingersR`
(one bone per hand for open/closed hands) and `eyelids` (blink, scale-driven) on every character. Extras:

- `esther`: `braid1 braid2 braid3` (chain from `head`), `cape1 cape2` (chain from `chest`), `diadem` (child of `head`).
- `king`: `crown` (child of `head`), `sceptre` (child of `handR`; tip = `sceptreTip`, a leaf bone at the knob),
  `ring` (child of `handR`; hidden after S04 by the director).
- `haman`: `cap` (child of `head`; slips in `shock`), `ring` (child of `handL`; shown from S04).

Faces: per-character face decal textures on UV1 like David & Goliath (`docs/david-goliath/RIG.md` face decals), one
PNG per expression in `docs/esther/design/faces/<char>_<expr>.png`, plus a separate eye-blink by `eyelids`.

## 5. Timeline (`assets/esther/timeline.json`)

Same format as David & Goliath §6 plus:
- top level `"cast": ["esther", "king", "haman"]`, `"set": "palace"`;
- `acting.y` (optional): base height of the actor for that mark (the king on the dais);
- `lines[].dir`: delivery note for the voice (emotion, pace, pauses), and `lines[].voice` (cast id);
- `props`: `[{"t": 20.1, "node": "king/ring", "show": false}]` (any node under an actor or the set; generic version
  of David & Goliath's desert stones);
- camera `target` may be any cast id, `all`, or a set node (`throne`, `table`).

The timeline is generated, never hand-edited: `docs/esther/storyboard/story.py` holds beats relative to each other,
and `tools/story/build_timeline.py esther` places them using the real voice durations
(`assets/esther/audio/voice/durations.json`, the longer of zh and en), so lines are never squeezed into fixed slots.

## 6. Set (`assets/esther/model/palace.glb`, root node `palace`)

Nodes (names are the contract): `dais` (3 steps, top height `set.daisTop` ≈ 0.07), `throne`, `columnL`, `columnR`,
`curtainL`, `curtainR` (with a gentle sway action `curtainSway`, looping), `brazierL`, `brazierR` (empty
`flameL`, `flameR` at the flame points for the FX), `carpet`, `floor`, `wallRelief`, `table` (banquet table with
`goblet1 goblet2 bowl bread`; hidden until S09 by `props`). Stage marks (art units, XZ): throne seat at (0.0, −0.30);
foot of the steps (0.0, −0.12); table at (0.22, 0.08). The set is UV-mapped to the page like the characters.

## 7. Audio

Voice −16 LUFS for TTS / −17 LUFS for recordings (LESSONS L50), music −20 LUFS, SFX peak −3 dBFS, 44.1 kHz mono WAV +
96 kbps MP3. Voices must sound like people telling a story to a child: chosen per character after listening tests,
per-line direction (`dir`), natural pauses, no robotic evenness. Lines are public paraphrases of Scripture, so cloud
TTS is allowed; nothing private goes into a TTS request.
