# Esther — art direction

Same family as David & Goliath (`docs/david-goliath/DESIGN.md`): Paper & Crayon look, rounded readable shapes, the
same head-to-body ratio, line weight and face style, so the two sheets read as one series. Every region a child can
colour is a closed shape at least 6 mm across on the A4 print. Period research: Achaemenid Persia (Susa, Persepolis):
fluted columns with bull capitals, glazed-brick friezes, winged guardian reliefs, purple and gold for royalty.
All characters and props are original designs.

## Characters

### Esther (front, centre; the hero)
- Long ivory robe with bell sleeves; a crimson shoulder cape that falls to mid-back; a gold sash and a light blue
  under-sash.
- Gold diadem with one raised point at the front; long dark hair in a single thick braid over the right shoulder.
- Two-strand gold bead necklace with a star-shaped lapis pendant (her "brave" accent colour).
- Expressions (face decals): `calm`, `worried`, `pray`, `brave`, `talk`, `joy`.
- Regions: skin, hair, robe, cape, sash, underSash, diadem, necklace, pendant, sandals.

### King Xerxes (back, on the throne steps)
- Long purple robe with a wide gold collar and a gold belt; tall flat-topped purple crown with three gold bands and
  small rosettes; short rounded dark beard with a few curl rows (kinder than a long square beard).
- Golden sceptre: a staff with a round knob and small wing shapes under it. The sceptre is its own colour region and
  its own prop bone (it is held out in S08).
- Expressions: `stern`, `listen`, `smile`, `surprised`, `angry`.
- Regions: skin, beard, robe, collar, belt, crown, crownBands, sceptre, shoes.

### Haman (right side)
- Wine-red robe with mustard-yellow panels and cuffs; dark teal round cap with a gold rim; thin pointed beard and
  moustache; a large signet ring (the king's ring, given in S04).
- Sly, not frightening: big eyebrows, small smirk; when found out he shrinks and his cap slips.
- Expressions: `smug`, `sly`, `shocked`, `scared`.
- Regions: skin, beard, robe, panels, cap, capRim, ring, shoes.

### Mordecai
Voice only (es_07). Not on the sheet: three figures already fill the A4 art rectangle.

## Set (the stage on the page)
- Throne dais: three wide steps and a throne with a tall back and lion-paw feet, at the back centre.
- Two fluted columns with twin-bull capitals, one at each side of the dais.
- Crimson curtains hung between the columns, tied back with gold cords.
- Two bronze braziers with flames (FX) at the front corners of the dais.
- A low banquet table (S09-S10) with goblets, a fruit bowl and bread; it slides in from the side in S09.
- Floor: a carpet with a simple border pattern (colourable) on pale stone tiles.
- Backdrop relief (printed, low): a winged guardian on a glazed-brick wall, light enough to colour.

## Light and mood per part
- S01-S02 warm gold interior light, brazier glow.
- S03-S07 cooler, darker (evening blue through the curtains), braziers flicker; tension.
- S08 a warm gold beam on the sceptre the moment it is held out.
- S09-S10 candle-warm banquet; S10 a quick cold flash when the truth is told.
- S11-S12 bright, warm, confetti and golden rays.

## Music direction (original score)
- Palace theme: D Dorian, harp-like plucked strings, soft frame drum, low string pad.
- Tension (S03-S07, S10): D Phrygian dominant (Hijaz), darbuka pattern, low strings, a reed lead.
- Sceptre moment (S08): suspended chord resolving to D major, bell sparkle.
- Celebration (S11-S12): D major, quick hand drums, clapping, flute melody.
