# The King Who Serves — music and sound effects

Generated by `tools/story/music_lastsupper.py` from the timeline (see "Which timeline"). Do not hand-edit the audio
files; change the script and rerun it:

```
python3 tools/story/music_lastsupper.py                           # music + sfx + preview + the tables below
python3 tools/story/music_lastsupper.py --skip-music --skip-sfx   # preview only (e.g. after new voice clips)
python3 tools/story/music_lastsupper.py --only towel,serve         # re-render some cues, keep the others' rows
python3 tools/story/music_lastsupper.py --stems                   # also print per-stem levels (for balancing)
python3 tools/story/music_lastsupper.py --spec /tmp/ls_spec       # also write a spectrogram PNG of every output
```

Same toolchain and shape as `music_miracles.py` (`mido numpy scipy`, `fluid-synth`, `ffmpeg`, macOS
`gs_instruments.dls`); imports `music_kit.py`, `music_polish.py` and `voice_margin.py` without changing them, and
imports `JESUS` / `JESUS_HARM` from `music_miracles.py` so both packs play one Jesus theme (if that theme is ever edited,
rerun both scripts). A full run takes about one minute. Scratch: `/tmp/lastsupper_music/` (emptied per cue, removed at
exit). Seeds are per cue / effect (`lastsupper/<id>`), so reruns are identical. Engine and licence: our own synthesis
(FluidSynth + the macOS DLS bank, numpy DSP), as in every pack (R7). The tables between the `<!-- …:start/end -->`
markers are rewritten by the script.

## Listen first

Nobody has listened yet; everything below was judged by measurement (1 s RMS profiles, band energy, spectrograms,
loop checks) and the voice-over-bed engine model. Please listen in this order (times are the zh play in
`preview_mix.mp3`):

1. **`finale` (210 – 231 s)**: the King march (flute, soft trumpet, oud) with the servant melody augmented under it
   (horn + cellos). The two-themes-together harmony (`FINALE_HARM`) is my own fit and was not heard; if anything
   clashes, it is bar 3 (Bm–A under a held E in the horn). The cue swells into the held D chord, then the `hee_haw`.
2. **`argue` (84 – 112 s)**: each disciple's motif cuts in under his own line (James clarinet, John oud, Peter tuba,
   Andrew soft clarinet), the tuba stomp after "doesn't count!" and the sour stab before the towel. The likeliest to
   read as clutter rather than comedy. Margins are fine (≥ 10 dB) but it is busy under dense dialogue.
3. **`prayer_house` (59 – 84 s)**: the firm chord on `table_tip` (the loudest moment of the score: −12 dB RMS at
   1-2 s into the cue) must feel strong, not scary; then the King theme in D minor on horn, then the tender children's
   choir from the golden rays. `market_bustle` keeps looping under this whole cue (see "Weak").
4. **`not_my_feet` (134 – 163 s)**: pizz scurry + stab on "Never!", the slide whistle (GM whistle, pitch-bent up an
   octave) at the end of "wash all of me!", the warm G resolve on Jesus' laugh (dipped 4 dB, it was the only line
   under 9 dB), and the tender cello for Judas.
5. **`towel` → `serve` → `supper`**: the servant theme (Jesus theme a fourth lower, slow) must feel like a hush; the
   towel opens very quietly (−35 dB RMS for 3 s) on purpose after the argue stab.
6. **`hosanna`**: the King march itself; check it is recognisably the Jesus theme of Miracles in a parade.
7. **SFX `hee_haw`, `crowd_far`, `market_bustle`, `table_tip`, `coins_scatter`, `dove_flutter`**: synthesised; judge
   whether the bray reads as a donkey (comic, not creepy), the crowd as a crowd, the wings as doves.
8. `preview_mix.mp3` start to end for balance.

## Which timeline

Cues are **composed for the play as the phone runs it in Chinese** (`voice_margin.lang_timeline("lastsupper", "zh")`,
230.9 s at the last run). It is the shortest play, so no cadence is cut off by the next cue. In-cue hits are looked
up by line id and event. Each file is long enough for its **longest** section across the zh play, the en play
(255.3 s) and `assets/lastsupper/timeline.json` (260.5 s, the longer clip per line) × 1.3 + 0.6 s (`longestSpan` in
`cues.json`); past its cadence each cue continues into a quiet loop section on its final chord (`[loopStart,
loopEnd)`, sample-exact, identical tiles). **Rerun this script after any re-time.**

## Formats (CONTRACT section 7)

- `assets/lastsupper/audio/music/<cue>.wav` and `audio/sfx/<id>.wav`: 44.1 kHz, mono, 16-bit PCM, plus a 96 kbps mono
  `.mp3` next to each.
- Music: the composed span at −20.5 LUFS, with a little range inside the contract's −20 ±1: `towel` −20.9, `supper`
  −21.0, `finale` −20.0, `market` −19.7, `hosanna` −19.6. Loop beds 4.5 dB under their cue. True peak ≤ −1 dBTP.
  Below 45 Hz removed; the speech pocket (−1.5 dB at 900 Hz, −3.5 dB at 2.4 kHz); the score sits 7 dB down under the
  story and swells on `table_tip` and on the finale's last chord (`music_polish.underscore`). `DIPS`: extra shallow
  dips under ls_04 (hosanna), ls_26 (not_my_feet), ls_31 (supper).
- SFX: sample peak −3.0 dBFS; `gainDb` in `mix.json` is the recommended playback gain.
- `assets/lastsupper/audio/preview_mix.mp3`: the zh play (36/36 lines) with music and SFX as `StoryDirector` mixes them.

## The themes

One melody, three faces, all from the Miracles `JESUS` theme (D major: A-B-D / E…D / F#-E-D-B / A-B-D …):

- **King theme** (`KING`): bars 1-4 as a bright march with dotted rhythms, flute + a soft trumpet over frame drum.
  `road` (hint), `hosanna` (in full), `prayer_house` (in D minor on horn), `finale`.
- **Servant theme** (`servant()`): the same melody a fourth lower (G major), slow, solo flute and harp. `towel`,
  `not_my_feet` (pizz, cello), `serve`, `my_turn` (bouncy oud, staccato).
- **Together** (`finale`): the King march on top, the servant melody augmented ×2 underneath (horn + cellos) over
  `FINALE_HARM` (D | Em G | Bm A | A D).

## Weak or unverified

- **Nobody has listened.** All judgements are measurements; see "Listen first".
- **`market_bustle` under `prayer_house`**: CONTRACT 7 says stop it at S06, so it loops (at −21 dB) through the tables
  tipping, the healing and the children singing. If the lead wants the market gone once the tables fall, change
  `LOOP_STOP_CUE` to `prayer_house` (one word) and rerun with `--skip-music`.
- **English timing**: composed for the zh play. In the en play `dove_flutter` lands 2.9 s, `heart_glow` 2.2 s,
  `golden_rays` 2.0 s and the first `hee_haw` 1.8 s later than the gesture written for them (the road bray falls after
  the cue's cadence, inside the loop bed). The en play's longer sections are covered by the loop beds.
- **The "oud" is a nylon guitar** and the slide whistle is the GM whistle bent up an octave (no real slide whistle
  in the bank). Same bank limit as Miracles.
- **Low end on phones**: `prayer_house` has 25 % of its energy below 150 Hz (the firm chord), `argue` 21 % (tuba),
  `stones` 20 %, `my_turn` 19 %; the rest ≤ 13 %. Weight was moved into horn / tremolo strings / pizz, but a phone
  will still thin the firm chord.
- **Very little above 2 kHz** (≤ 4 % in every cue): warm bank + low-passes + speech pocket. Intended; may sound dull on
  a good speaker.
- **`crowd_far` loop**: the two halves differ by 1.0 dB (`market_bustle` 0.8 dB); seams are clean (x0.3).
- **Re-timing**: if the lead's staging changes act timings, rerun the script; hits follow the events and files are
  sized with a 30 % margin.
