# Gabriel — art direction

**The finest character in DoodLive.** Same family as Esther / Ruth / Daniel (Paper & Crayon, rounded readable shapes,
decal faces) but pushed to hero quality: a real sculpt, not primitives. Every colourable region ≥ 6 mm on the A4 print
(easy here: he fills the sheet). All designs original; the reference only lends costume logic and mood.

## Gabriel

**Build and face.** A young man in his twenties, heroic proportions but friendly (about 7 heads tall, broad
shoulders, narrow waist, long legs). Height **0.62** art-width units (much larger than the other packs' 0.34: he is
alone on the page and the hero of the shelf). Strong clean jaw, straight nose, warm big eyes (larger than realistic,
in the house style), expressive brows, a ready smile. **Hair:** shoulder-length, thick, wavy, swept back from a side
parting, in big sculpted locks (not strands) that read as shapes on the sheet.

**Armour and clothes** (from top):
- **Pauldrons:** big, rounded, two overlapping gold plates on each shoulder, a round boss on the top plate.
- **Collar:** a gold V-shaped gorget with a pointed front tab.
- **Cuirass:** ivory / cream moulded leather with sculpted chest and abdomen, a centre seam, thin gold trim at the
  edges. Ivory long sleeves under the pauldrons.
- **Vambraces:** gold, from wrist to below the elbow, with a scalloped / pointed top edge.
- **Belt and sash:** a wide burnt-orange sash wound at the waist, its long end hanging in front to the knees
  (a tabard panel that swings), plus a narrow gold belt.
- **Skirt / legs:** ivory leggings; short ivory skirt panels at the hips.
- **Greaves:** gold, from ankle to knee with a pointed knee cop; gold sandal-boots.
- **Sword** (always visible, sheathed): at the left hip, gold cross-guard with a round blue gem, ivory scabbard.
- **Trumpet** (prop, hidden until S05): a long slender golden herald trumpet with a flared bell (~0.30 long).
- **No halo.** Light comes from the effects.

**Wings** — the signature. Huge, white, layered and believable: three rows of coverts over long primaries and
secondaries, individual feather shapes with a central shaft line and soft tips, slightly warm white with a pale gold
underside tint. Spread pose (rest / sheet pose): span ≈ **1.0** (fills the art width), tips raised above the head.
Folded pose: tucked behind the back, tips near the calves. Built so the feathers fan open and closed through the wing
bones (CONTRACT §3), not as one rigid plate.

**Expressions** (face decals): `calm`, `smile` (default), `talk`, `kind` (soft smile, brows up), `joy` (open smile),
`announce` (mouth open wide, eyes bright — trumpet / proclamation), `blow` (cheeks puffed, eyes closed, for the
trumpet), `wink`.

**Regions on the sheet:** skin, hair, pauldrons, collar, cuirass, sleeves, vambraces, sash, belt, skirt / leggings,
greaves, sword hilt, scabbard, wings (two regions: coverts and long feathers, so a child can colour them differently).

## Set: the city of light (`heaven`)

- **Cloud floor:** the whole page is a sea of soft, lumpy golden-white clouds (big rounded puffs, not noise), a
  little higher at the edges so the centre reads as a stage.
- **Gate of heaven** (back centre): a tall arched gate in a long white-gold wall with columns, a glowing sun-disc /
  rosette over the arch, a stair of 5 wide steps coming down to the clouds; wall wings to both sides with towers.
- **Floating islets:** two small floating rock-and-garden islands with a tree and a waterfall wisp (back left, back
  right), hanging above the cloud floor.
- **Front clouds:** a few big cloud puffs framing the front corners (keep the centre clear).

## Light and mood
Golden hour in heaven throughout: warm amber key from the back (the gate), soft fill, glowing rim on the wings.
`heaven_rays` (god rays from the gate) all play; `descend_light` in S02; the trumpet call in S05 is the brightest moment.

## Music direction (original score; reference mood: epic, warm cinematic orchestra with choir)
A **Gabriel theme**: a noble rising herald motif (a 4-note call, rising fourth then fifth) that the trumpet plays in S05.
- `heaven` choir "ah" pad, slow strings, harp shimmer; awe; the theme hinted on horns.
- `descend` harp glissandi, swelling strings and choir, a soft timpani roll; ends as he lands.
- `gentle` warm strings and celesta, intimate; the theme softly on flute.
- `story` light narrative strings, pizzicato, a little sense of travel.
- `fanfare` the full herald call: trumpets, timpani, choir, cymbal swell; triumphant and joyful, not military.
- `finale` full orchestra and choir statement of the theme, rising, ending on a bright held chord.
