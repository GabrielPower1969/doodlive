# Gabriel — the archangel (story pack, replaces the old trumpet-angel book)

The first book on the shelf. The old procedural angel (`pipeline/angel_build.py`, `Angel/`, sheet `doodlive-angel-A4`
and the three themed sheets) stays in the app untouched, so printed old sheets still scan; the shelf's first book
becomes this pack. Owner's brief (2026-11-14): re-render and adapt the archangel Gabriel after a reference viewing of a
CBN Superbook episode (S01E01), "the most finely sculpted character of the whole work", music in that spirit.

Source: Luke 1:11-38, Daniel 9:21-23, Luke 2:10, Isaiah 41:10 (public domain). Lines are child-friendly spoken
paraphrases with their verses; nothing is quoted from the film. Audience 4-10. What the reference viewing lends:
costume logic (golden armour over ivory leather, orange sash, huge white wings), the golden-cloud heaven with a
shining gate, and an epic-but-warm orchestral sound with choir. Character, face, set, words and music are original.

Title: **大天使加百列 / Gabriel the Archangel**.
