# Exodus — production contract (story pack #5)

The brief every role works from. Same rules as `docs/ruth/CONTRACT.md` (and through it Esther and David & Goliath)
unless this file says otherwise: units, UV = page position, audio formats, "only Integration runs Unity", nobody
commits, nobody touches another role's files. Story and lines: `docs/exodus/STORY.md` +
`docs/exodus/storyboard/story.py`; look: `docs/exodus/DESIGN.md`; architecture: `docs/STORY-ARCHITECTURE.md`,
`docs/STORY-ENGINE.md`.

**Ruth (pack #3) is the template**, Esther (pack #2) the older example. Every Ruth pipeline script (`pipeline/ru_*.py`,
`pipeline/story_mesh.py`, `tools/story/*.py`, `StoryFxRuth*.cs`) and doc (`docs/ruth/*.md`) is a working,
device-tested example. Start from a copy, keep what works, generalise shared helpers rather than forking them when
that is cheap. Read `docs/LESSONS.md` before you start (L44 onward especially).

**Another pack (Daniel, `docs/daniel/`, prefix `da_`) is being built in the same repository at the same time.** Touch
only the files your role owns below. Shared files (`pipeline/story_mesh.py`, `tools/story/make_sheet.py`,
`tools/story/voice.py`, `DGFxEffects.cs`, `tools/make_fonts.py`, `assets/stories.json`, `assets/library.json`) get
**additive** changes only (new functions / new entries, existing behaviour byte-identical), and you re-run the other
packs' check after touching one. Never run Unity, never use the phone, never `git` anything but `status`/`diff`.

Quality bar (owner review 2026-11-13/14): David & Goliath was "average", its lines stiff and its voices "sounded like
AI". Esther raised the bar, Ruth raised it again. Exodus must match Ruth and add **spectacle**: the sea parting is
the hero moment of the whole app so far.

## 3. Skeleton

Humanoid bones exactly as Ruth (`root hips spine chest neck head jaw shoulderL/R upperArmL/R forearmL/R handL/R
thighL/R shinL/R footL/R toeL/R fingersL fingersR eyelids`), A-pose, facing +Z. Extras:

- `moses`: `beard1 beard2` (chain from `jaw`/`head`, the beard tip sways), `mantleL mantleR` (from `chest`, the open
  mantle's front edges), `mantleBack` (from `chest`, the back drape), `staff` (child of `handR`; tip leaf bone
  `staffTip` at the top knot).
- `pharaoh`: `nemes1L nemes1R` (the lappets on the chest), `nemesBack` (the back tail), `chariot` (child of `root`,
  the chariot body + right wheel + pole; hidden by default), `wheelL` (child of `chariot`, the left wheel; hidden in
  S12 when it pops off).
- `miriam`: `scarf1 scarf2` (chain from `head`), `braid1 braid2` (chain from `head`), `tambourine` (child of `handL`;
  hidden by default).

Props are rigid meshes parented to their bone. The director shows / hides them by `prop` events
(`"moses/staff"`, `"pharaoh/chariot"`, `"pharaoh/wheelL"`, `"miriam/tambourine"`): the node name after the slash is the
bone name **and** the mesh object (`pharaoh_chariot` mesh found under bone `chariot`, as Ruth's `ruth_sheaf`). The
chariot floor top is at y ≈ 0.035; while the chariot shows, Pharaoh's feet stand on it (animation `ride` lifts the
hips by that much; the chariot itself rests on the page).

Faces: decal textures on UV1 as Ruth, one PNG per expression in `docs/exodus/design/faces/<char>_<expr>.png`, plus a
blink by `eyelids`. Expressions: DESIGN.md. Moses's bushy white eyebrows carry his acting; Pharaoh's kohl eyes and thin
brows are the comic engine (big range: smug ↔ panic ↔ soggy).

## 7. Audio

Voice −16 LUFS for TTS / −17 LUFS for recordings, music −20 LUFS, SFX peak −3 dBFS, 44.1 kHz mono WAV + 96 kbps MP3,
as Ruth. Lines are public paraphrases of Scripture, so cloud TTS is allowed; nothing private goes into a request.

Voices: people telling a story to a child (Ruth §7 is the brief). Moses must sound old but strong (not frail), and
change across the story: hesitant and shy in S03-S04, firm from S05 on. Pharaoh is a comic baritone with a big range
(pompous, squealing, sly, sorrowful in S07, soggy). God: calm, deep, warm, unhurried, with a light hall reverb added
in post; never booming or scary. Miriam: bright, quick, warm; her S13 chant is rhythmic, half-sung over the `song` cue
tempo (6/8, dotted-quarter = 112, bar = 1.0714 s; ex_33 on bar 2, ex_34 on bar 10; grid in MUSIC.md / cues.json). `people`: 4-6 voices together.

Music cues (ids): `slavery desert bush palace plagues passover exodus chase sea song finale` (`cues.json` with loop
points as Ruth). SFX ids: `bricks sigh_crowd sheep_bells bush_whoosh hiss sparkle sting croak buzz hail
crickets_night cheer chariot wind sea_rush wheel_pop splash drip` (`mix.json`).

## 8. Effects ids (played by id through `DGFx.Play`)

Reused: `dust_road`, `sunset_glow`, `night`, `prayer_glow`, `sparkle`, `confetti`, `golden_rays`. New for Exodus
(`StoryFxExodus.cs`, registered like `RuthFx.Defs`):

- `bush_fire` (node; white-gold flames licking the bush with a soft violet halo and slow rising sparks, light on the
  actors nearby; the bush mesh is never charred; loop for `dur`).
- `snake` (from a hand: the staff "falls" and a green-brown snake, ~0.16 long, procedural wriggle on the page in front
  of Moses for `dur`, then it straightens up into the hand with a sparkle; the director's prop events hide/show the
  real staff around it).
- `river_red` (node: tints the `nileWater` material toward dark red over 0.6 s, holds, back over 1 s).
- `frogs` (stage: 10-14 small green frogs hop in from the river side in arcs across the floor, sit, hop off at the
  end; avoid actors' feet), `frog_on_head` (one frog lands on a bone and sits there, a croak bounce, hops off at the end).
- `flies` (node: a small buzzing dark swarm circling for `dur`), `hail` (stage: white hailstones dropping and bouncing,
  a brief cool tint).
- `door_glow` (node: warm soft glow on the door marks and the doorways, pulses gently).
- `pillar_cloud` / `pillar_fire` (node: a tall soft column, 0.5 high, slowly swirling; cloud is white-grey glowing,
  fire is warm orange with embers; fades in/out).
- `sea_wind` (node: streaks of wind and spray blowing across the sea, robes and particles lean; a cool tint).
- `sea_part` (node `egypt.sea`: over `dur` hides `seaWaterLane` with a splitting ripple, raises `seaWallBack` /
  `seaWallFront` from their flat rest to full height with a foamy crest and spray, and keeps them standing (gentle
  surface motion on the walls, caustic shimmer on the sea-bed) until `sea_close`).
- `sea_close` (node: the walls collapse inward with a big splash and spray, the lane water returns, ripples settle;
  ends in the rest state).
- `splash` (on a bone: a burst of water drops and a ring on the ground), `drips` (on a bone: water drips from the
  head for `dur`).

`sea_part` / `sea_close` must also work when the director fast-forwards (`BeginAt` jumps into S12 or S13): expose a
`SetState(parted: bool)` the director can call, or make the FX state a pure function of elapsed time as Ruth's `night` does.
