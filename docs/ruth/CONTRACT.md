# Ruth — production contract (story pack #3)

The brief every role works from. Same rules as `docs/esther/CONTRACT.md` (and through it `docs/david-goliath/CONTRACT.md`)
unless this file says otherwise: units, UV = page position, audio formats, "only Integration runs Unity", nobody
commits, nobody touches another role's files. Story and lines: `docs/ruth/STORY.md` + `docs/ruth/storyboard/story.py`;
look: `docs/ruth/DESIGN.md`; architecture: `docs/STORY-ARCHITECTURE.md`, `docs/STORY-ENGINE.md`.

**Esther (pack #2) is the template.** Every Esther pipeline script (`pipeline/es_*.py`, `pipeline/story_mesh.py`,
`tools/story/*.py`, `StoryFxEsther*.cs`) and doc (`docs/esther/*.md`) is a working, device-tested example. Start from a
copy, keep what works, and generalise shared helpers rather than forking them when that is cheap. Read
`docs/LESSONS.md` before you start (L44-L50 especially).

Quality bar (owner review 2026-11-13/14): David & Goliath's set was "average", its lines stiff and its voices "sounded
like AI, no human touch". Esther raised the bar and was approved. Ruth must be **finer again**: faces with more life,
cloth that hangs and moves, a set that feels like a real place with many small details, voices that sound like people.

## 3. Skeleton

Humanoid bones exactly as Esther (`root hips spine chest neck head jaw shoulderL/R upperArmL/R forearmL/R handL/R
thighL/R shinL/R footL/R toeL/R fingersL fingersR eyelids`), A-pose, facing +Z. Extras:

- `ruth`: `scarf1 scarf2` (chain from `head`, the scarf tail down the back), `sheaf` (child of `chest`, the barley
  bundle held in both arms; hidden by default), `stalks` (child of `handR`, a small handful; hidden by default).
- `naomi`: `shawl1 shawl2` (chain from `head`), `staff` (child of `handR`), `baby` (child of `chest`, swaddled baby
  cradled in both arms; hidden by default).
- `boaz`: `cloakL cloakR` (one bone each, from `chest`, the open robe's front panels), `headcloth` (from `head`, the
  cloth's back drape), `stalks` (child of `handR`, a big handful; hidden by default).

Props are rigid meshes parented to their bone. The director shows / hides them by `prop` events
(`"ruth/sheaf"`, `"naomi/baby"`, ...): the node name after the slash is the bone name **and** the mesh object
(`ruth_sheaf` mesh is found under bone `sheaf`; follow Esther's `king/ring` precedent exactly).

Faces: decal textures on UV1 as Esther, one PNG per expression in `docs/ruth/design/faces/<char>_<expr>.png`, plus a
blink by `eyelids`. Expressions: DESIGN.md. More life than Esther: eyebrows that differ per expression, a hint of
nose, cheek blush, mouth shapes that read at phone distance.

## 7. Audio

Voice −16 LUFS for TTS / −17 LUFS for recordings, music −20 LUFS, SFX peak −3 dBFS, 44.1 kHz mono WAV + 96 kbps MP3,
as Esther. Lines are public paraphrases of Scripture, so cloud TTS is allowed; nothing private goes into a request.

The voice brief is the hardest one: **people telling a story to a child**, not text being read. Esther's measured
approach (Qwen3-TTS best-of-N with acting notes for Chinese; phrased edge-tts for English) is the floor, not the
ceiling. Things that make a voice sound human: real breaths before emotional lines, varied pause lengths, pitch that
moves with the meaning, a smile or a catch in the voice where the `dir` asks for it, interjections (唉, 哇, 咦, 嘘) that
sound like sounds, not words. Distinct, consistent timbres: Naomi must sound older than Ruth.

Music cues (ids): `road_sad journey field boaz sneaky home night wedding lullaby finale` (`cues.json` with loop points
as Esther). SFX ids: `soft_chime birds rustle stalks_drop crickets_dusk crickets_night sparkle cheer tambourine
baby_coo` (`mix.json`). `baby_coo` may be a soft twinkle if a synthetic baby would sound wrong.
